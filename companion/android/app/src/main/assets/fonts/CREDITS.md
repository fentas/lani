Kalam comes from Google Fonts (https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Kalam; source https://github.com/itfoundry/kalam). The
app bundles a subset: the Latin letters (Basic Latin, Latin-1, Latin Extended A, B and Additional: č š ž ć đ, ä ö ü ß, à è
é ì ò ù and the rest), the punctuation and quotes (»« „“ «» ‘’ – — …); the Devanagari is left out. The font names and
the licence notice inside the files are kept. Kalam has no Reserved Font Name.
