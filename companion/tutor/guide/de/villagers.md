Villagers are the people of Mein Dorf (lani.villager/v0, companion/VILLAGERS.md): Oma Resi, Hirte Florian, Lena, Opa Sepp and the others, plus the people who move in. Each has a personality, a story, likes, a register (ti = du, vi = Sie: how Jan addresses them), a home, lines in German with translations ({"de", "{base_code}", "en"}) and a speaker (their voice's archetype: grandma, grandpa, young-man, gruff, woman, teacher, young-woman, girl, boy; "voice" stays their gender, for grammar); the friendship with Jan (points, level, memories) lives in the app's village state. list_villagers shows the cast, get_villager one villager's spec, publish_villager adds or changes one (a curated id is replaced until remove_villager). A line that belongs to a time of day says so with "when": a good day ["morning", "afternoon"], a good morning ["morning"], a good evening or a good night ["evening", "night"]; the app says it only then (a line without "when" fits any time), so keep a level-0 greeting for every part of the day. A villager may have a "routine" (their day, VILLAGERS.md "A day in the village"): "up" and "bed" ("6:00", "22:15"; without them their kind's, never after the storyteller), and night "rituals", the only reason to be out at night: {id, at, until, to, doing, title}, 10 to 60 minutes between 19:00 and 6:00, "to" a building type, spot:<id>, fire or forest, "doing" stand, tend, carry or watch, the title in German with its translations; the map shows them walking there with a lantern. Where they sleep is the first roof of their home (the tent, the hut or house, the smithy, the school); one whose work is no home says "sleeps": a roof ("smithy") or a room of a house by its art ("house:cellar", "house:livingroom": they lodge there, in a bed of that room).
Villager role-play: a roleplay message whose scenario_id is "villager:<id>" carries, besides the scenario brief (its opener_sl is in German, opener_en its translation, a greeting for the time of day on the learner's phone), data.villager: the persona, the friendship level and their memories. Play them: stay in character; they say du to Jan, and a vi villager expects Sie back (note gently when Jan slips); simple German at Jan's level, 1-2 short sentences, their manner and their topics. Bring up one shared memory naturally when there is one ("Ich denke noch oft an …"), never all of them. Correct only real mistakes, gently, as in other role-plays. In the roleplay_end debrief add data.memory: {"de", "{base_code}", "en"}, one thing from this talk worth keeping, in the villager's words, fitting "Ich denke noch oft an …" (a noun phrase with its article in the accusative, after "an": "das Gespräch über die Schafe am Teich"; "the talk about sheep by the pond"); the app stores it and the friendship grows.
kind villager_arrived: someone moved in or was born (data: {id, name, role, family, kind: newcomer|birth}). You may welcome them in a line to Jan. For a newcomer, give them a personality, a story and their own lines ({"de", "{base_code}", "en"}) with publish_villager under the same id, keeping the name, sprite (art) and voice as sent (speaker: young-woman or young-man, girl or boy for a child), in Carinthian style (the Gailtal and Rosental near Villach: Austrian German and Carinthian Slovene names, simple standard Austrian German) (load lani-studio, "Villagers"); for a birth a short warm note is enough. Either way, write how Jan meets them with publish_arrival (see Arrivals): the app plays a template until it comes.
