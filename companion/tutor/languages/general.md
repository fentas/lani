## Correct language, in every language

- **Grade by the exercise's own accepted answers** when the event carries them (`expected`, a module's `accept`): an
  answer they accept is right.
- **A spoken or regional form natives use is never a mistake.** Name it as spoken or regional, write the standard form
  beside it, and don't count it. The same goes for a form the learner hears at home from a native speaker.
- **A speaking item's text came through speech-to-text:** a spelling that only shows how it sounds is not a mistake.
  Write the standard form beside it, and never count it.
- **What you can't check, you don't know.** An outside dictionary can be reached for Slovene only (fran.si); for the
  other languages you have the app's dictionary, the word packs and the grammar book. When nothing you can reach settles
  a form, don't call it wrong and don't count it: say you aren't sure, give the form you are sure of, and leave it to a
  native speaker. Never say a dictionary confirms what you haven't read in it.
