# Built: Slovene word packs for B1 and B2 (packs-sl)

*Built 2026-11-03, branch `packs-sl` (followups-14, section 7). Jan: "I want to have app finished to release". Packs
stopped at A2, and a B2 aim asks 1,000 words learned by Trg and 2,000 by Mesto (GAME.md, "Words learned per age").
Everything here is machine-written (Claude) in Slovene, English, German and Italian and waits for a native speaker's
check (the list comes first). The Slovene was settled against fran.si (SP2001) and Sloleks, one request at a time.*

## For the native check

What a dictionary can't settle: register, naturalness, who says what. At most two lines a pack; every pack's `review`
says it is machine-written.

**What the sources settled** (SP2001 on fran.si, `FilteredDictionaryIds=134`, and Sloleks, each word's URL in its
supplement entry's `source` or its forms-book table's `note`; 1,403 words looked up, one request at a time):

- *dagnja* is `redk.` in SP2001 (https://fran.si/iskanje?View=1&Query=dagnja&FilteredDictionaryIds=134), the
  dictionary's word is *klapavica*: the pack teaches klapavica and names dagnje as the coast's and the menus' word.
- *Državni zbor* is a name, with its capital (https://fran.si/iskanje?View=1&Query=dr%C5%BEavni%20zbor&FilteredDictionaryIds=134).
- *trajnostni*: SP2001 has the sense only in this form; *trajnosten* is `redk.` "lasting" (…&Query=trajnosten).
- *urgenca* for A&E is `zdrav. žarg.` (…&Query=urgenca): the note says so. *imeti temperaturo* for a fever is `knj. pog.`
  (…&Query=temperatura): the note says so and the example says *povišana temperatura*.
- *smeti*: Sloleks has the noun as *smet* (the plural taught); *žegnanje* is standard, SP2001 glosses it *proščenje*.
- *žerjav*: `živ.` the bird, `neživ.` the machine (…&Query=%C5%BEerjav): the forms book's accusative is the machine's.
- *robot* is `živ.` (…&Query=robot), *izvajalec*, *dobavitelj*, *vodnik*, *navijač* `člov.`: animate; the charger
  *polnilec* is a thing (SP2001's *polnilec 1* is a person).
- *kmet*: plural *kmetje*, *kmeti* `redk.`; *vodja* and *računovodja* decline both ways (-e and -a: SP2001 "vódja -e
  in vódja -a"); *strah*, *mah*, *glas*, *plaz*, *led*, *rod*, *jez*, *tram* have both genitives (-a, -u).
- Not in Sloleks: *štirna* (SSKJ `nar. primorsko`, a cistern) and *suhozid*; their tables follow the regular declension.
- The rest: every pack word is in Sloleks with the gender the pack gives (the plural-only headwords aside: *volitve*,
  *nadure*, *pogajanja*, *testenine*, *mediji*, *poročila*, *soline*, *lignji*, written by hand or under their singular
  lemma). Sense-level qualifiers SP2001 gives (a word's other, older or slang sense: *plastika*, *dobiti*, *posel 1* a
  servant) don't touch the meaning taught.

## How

- **Two writers.** A helper wrote 21 of the B1 packs to the model (`pri-zdravniku`), the lead the other 5 B1 and all 29 B2
  packs. A brief fixed each pack's theme and giver, so the writers' words didn't meet.
- **`companion/lexicon/tools/sl_packs_check.py`** (fast, no network): duplicates against every Slovene pack, a lemma a
  pack has in another form, new prompt clashes, field lengths, the review's 200 characters, the cast, the example
  words no dictionary entry knows, and the distinct count. `--existing` lists every word the packs teach.
- **`companion/lexicon/tools/sl_sources.py`**: SP2001 on fran.si (`FilteredDictionaryIds=134`) and Sloleks
  (`viri.cjvt.si/sloleks`, its search and headword pages), one request at a time, at least 3 s apart, every answer
  cached (`.cache/sl-sources`, not committed). `sloleks` fetches the forms, `check` reports a word neither source has,
  a gender or plural Sloleks doesn't give, and SP2001's qualifiers; `auto` finds the example words that are forms of
  a pack word the dictionary lacks; `supplement --write` writes the entries.
- **`companion/lexicon/tools/sl_pack_fmt.py`**: the packs' layout, so a pack edited by a script reads as one by hand.

## The supplement (companion/lexicon/sl.extra.json)

- **1,093 new entries** (1,449 → 2,542; 646 nouns, 214 adjectives, 193 verbs, the rest adverbs, prepositions, names),
  12,027 forms, and forms merged into 39 older entries. Each new pack word the dictionary lacks has its whole Sloleks
  paradigm in the supplement's grammar labels, the pack's meanings in en, de, it, and its `source`: SP2001's head with its
  URL (982) and the Sloleks headword's (1,032). The other words the examples use came from 508 records
  (`companion/lexicon/tools/sl_extra_words/`: written by hand, or made by `auto` and `wiki-gaps` for the forms of a pack
  word the dictionary lacks), 26 of them forms by hand where a dictionary's lemma lacked them.
- **A form that is another lemma's** in the dictionary or the supplement is left out of the new entry (about 80: *pregleda*
  is also pregledati's), so a tap on it keeps the reading it had. *mlaj* gets no entry: the case lens derives it, and the
  smoke tests that.
- **The forms book** (`sl.forms.json`): 63 noun tables (90 → 153) from Sloleks with SP2001's head, for the nouns whose
  paradigm the regular check can't confirm (a form left out as another word's, both genitives, the plural-only ones,
  žerjav's machine), and 129 people and animals in the animate list (98 → 227): the case trainer's "Sklanjaj!" can ask
  every new noun.
- **Coverage** (`lexicon-build --coverage --lang sl`): the content's different words 8,386 → 11,121; known to the
  dictionary and the supplement 81.4 % → 87.2 % of them, with the packs and names 84.2 % → 89.3 %; words none knows 1,323 →
  1,195. Every example word of the new packs is known (the cast's names aside). The case lens's unknown forms are 91
  (main: 9; the smoke wants fewer than 150).


## How packs reach a learner

- **The suggestion and the packs screen** come from packs-en's shared fix (cherry-picked, 3c5f3ba5 → 4f48c293):
  `PackSession.suggest` takes the learner's level first, then below it, then the nearest level up; the packs screen
  folds the curated packs two levels or more above the learner's under "Za pozneje · For later". An A1 learner meets
  A1, then A2 packs, and sees the B1 and B2 packs folded; a B1 learner gets B1 then B2.
- **An older app** (Teo's 0.2.698, a lagging phone) doesn't send `?packs=1`, so the bridge serves it the curated packs
  up to one level above the learner's, plus anything started: Jan's phone at A2 sees B1, not B2, until it updates.
- **The prints**: the sheets take a word's meaning from the packs in file order; packs-de's fix (9f52ee3e → 94cc55ff)
  puts B1+ packs after A1/A2, so no early word prints a B2 meaning. Printed in every base with the B1/B2 packs hidden and in (`school-prints --level all --html-only`, a copy of the tree
without them): the A1 and A2 sheets are the same; the B1 and B2 sheets gain meanings from the new packs (*pasti se* "to
graze" where it printed "to fall", *kmet* "farmer" for "peasant", *bati se* "to be afraid"). Against main, with the
supplement too, the A1 and A2 sheets differ in one word: unit 12's list gains *dopoldne* "in the morning", which no
entry knew.
- **Requests and buildings are unchanged**: the village's quests name A-level packs as topics, BuildingWords names the
  buildings' packs, and a visit's request takes six words of the host's packs by giver or topic; none names a new pack.
  A request whose giver gives a B2 pack can draw its words (the giver's packs come first): the window's pace (one open
  request at a time) holds. **Not done here**: what counts as "started" (a pack whose word the learner looked up
  elsewhere) is packs-en's coming fix.
