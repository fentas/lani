# Built: every building of the Friuli village enterable, and every building's scene at its level

Jan, 2026-10-30: "teo upgraded the tower - now he can't enter it > can you build all scenes for all upgrades including slo
culture?" Teo, a child of about 7 learning Italian from German in the Friuli village (the Collio near Gorizia, his land
the coast, a Galaxy Tab S6), had upgraded his watchtower to level 2. Its card had no "🚪 Entra": a building's card shows
a way in only where the village's culture has a scene for it, and Friuli had none for the watchtower, the market, the
square, the bee garden, the church or the house's rooms but the kitchen. And a scene didn't show what an upgrade did: the
art drew the building the same at every level (the rooms changed up to level 3, then stood still).

This note is the round's part B (`places-it-buildings`): the Friuli scenes of the buildings, Friuli's own look for the arts
that still drew Slovenia in the Friuli village, and every building's scene at each of its five levels, in every culture.
The Slovene scenes of the village projects and the palisade are part A's (`places-sl`), the Friuli scenes of the projects
part C's (`places-it-projects`). Two helpers drew part of it, each on a branch merged into this one: H1 the market, the
square, the smithy's and the school's levels; H2 the bee garden, the church, the tent's and the field's levels (marked so in
the tables). The formats are companion/SCENES.md's.

`BuildingLevelsTest` (new) holds every building to it: the buildings and their arts come from every culture's scenes'
`from` (a new art joins as its scene does; every building but the palisade, whose scene is the gate's, must have one), in
the default culture and each culture whose look draws the art: each level differs from the one before in at least 300
pixels of the 240 × 160 canvas by day (a thing a child sees come with the upgrade, not noise), level 5 is the picture of
everything built, and what can be tapped at a level is what the level has. Its sheets: `app/build/scene-snapshots/levels/
<building>-<art>-{day,evening}.png`, a row a level, a column a culture. `SceneWorldRenderTest` no longer stages the rooms
(this test does) and `RoomLevelsRenderTest` renders the rooms at 1 to 5.

## How to use

Nothing to switch on. The scenes, packs and clues are content: a Friuli village's bridge serves them once the node's
checkout has them (the app bundles the clues and the looks with its next build; an older app draws Friuli's buildings in
the Slovene picture at every level and plays the scenes all the same: no new art id).

QA, with a synthetic Teo (never a real learner's data):

```
bun companion/bridge/test/fixtures/make-learner.ts <dir> --name Teo --target it --base de
LANI_DEV_DATA=<dir> LANI_ENV_FILE=/dev/null LANI_CULTURE=friuli LANI_CHILD=1 LANI_QA_SETUP_PAIR=it-de LANI_QA_LEARNER=Teo \
LANI_QA_FRIULI_SHOTS=<shots> flock /tmp/lani-emulator.lock companion/bin/qa --no-town --steps friuli-watchtower
```

The step makes the village a Trg (QA's hook `hearths:trg`: the watchtower at level 2), taps the tower on the map, goes in
by its card's "🚪 Entra", looks closer and on the tablet, plays a round of «Vedo, vedo» (the horn) and opens Davide's
lookout in the evening.
