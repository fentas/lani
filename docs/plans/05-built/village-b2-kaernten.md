# village-b2-kaernten: Kärnten's village to B2

Wave E, batch 2b, section 3 (followups-16.md), for Kärnten (German). Base main 1311eeb2a, main merged in since.

## For a native check first (machine-written, Claude)

Everything below is machine-written: the German, and the Slovene, English and Italian beside it. Nothing was reviewed by a native
speaker. Worth a native eye first:

- **The charcoal pile's words.** *Quandel* for the shaft in the middle, *Lösche* for the earth mixed with charcoal dust, *Scheiter*
  for logs, *ausziehen* for drawing the charcoal, *einsacken*, *der Meiler bricht durch*. The Slovene beside them (*stržen*,
  *kopišče*, *mešanica* for the Lösche, *jašek*), the Italian (*ciocchi*, *la piazzola*, *lo spiazzo*, *il camino*, *la Lösche* left in German).
- **Carinthian and Austrian words a native should judge in context.** *Schwaiberl* and its Slovene *škarje* (the Doppelharpfe's
  steps), the Harpfe and toplar terms, *Lavanttaler Bananenapfel*, *Stadel*, *heuer*, *Mesner*, *Erdäpfel*, *Most*, *Kirchtag*,
  *narrisch*, *Stückerl*. The supplement says what is spoken.
- **Wrong choices that must stay a learner's slip**, never what an adult there says (followups-12's last section). I took out
  weil with the verb second, als ob with the indicative, *größer wie*, the indicative in reported speech, *wegen dem*, the Perfekt
  for the Präteritum. What's left tests auxiliaries (*umgefallen hatte*, *hätten … gewesen*, *hätte alles verbrannt*), *repariert
  geworden*, *in Betracht genommen*, *stolz daran*, *an den Köhlern abhängig*, *darf* for *dürfte*, *als ob vermissen Sie*,
  *wenn der Meiler ist kalt*, and forms nobody says (*gesieht*, *gewordet*, *gewinnt*). Check these, *hätte alles verbrannt* and
  *für wem* first.
- **Puns in reactions** (a reaction may not say the right form, so some are jokes): Michl's «Geworden? Hm, ein Rechen wird doch nicht
  erwachsen», «Sammelnde Erde? Das wär was!», «Darauf gedacht? Das klingt, als hättest du dich draufgesetzt», «Hätten gewesen? Hm,
  jetzt bin ich wirklich beleidigt». Check that they read kindly, and for a child.
- **Michl's sayings** are his own, not proverbs: «Ein Meiler verzeiht dir nichts», «Ein Köhler ohne Holz ist wie ein Wirt ohne Gäste»,
  «wie ein Wirtshaus am Montag», «den Rauch lesen wie eine Zeitung». 
- **Facts added** (none contradicts VILLAGERS.md, the arrivals or the stories as far as I checked). Michl: he could have been a teacher
  in Villach, the priest of those days had spoken to his parents; his grandfather built the hut and carried the sacks to the ironworks;
  his mother watched the pile alone for ten nights when his father was ill, and prayed before a lighting; ten piles smoked in the
  valley in his childhood and he is the last one there; twelve sacks a pile; a professor from Graz measured four hundred degrees; a cart
  once burnt in the village. Translations of a helper's lines in Slovene may carry the helper's gender (the validator checks that in
  Slovene packs only).
- **The history.** The spot's B1 and B2 say Hüttenberg made iron in Roman times (as the A2 does), that ironworks owned whole forests
  and that coal ended the craft. General history, no Carinthian record read for the forests.
