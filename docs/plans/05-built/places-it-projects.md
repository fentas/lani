# Built: the Friuli village's projects to step into

Jan, 2026-10-30: "teo upgraded the tower - now he can't enter it > can you build all scenes for all upgrades including slo
culture?" Teo (a child of about seven learning Italian from German, the Friuli culture, a tablet) could step into his
tent, kitchen, field, smithy, school and mill, and nothing else his village had finished: of its thirteen village
projects only the mill had a scene. This part of the round (agent C, branch `places-it-projects`) writes the Friuli scenes
for the projects whose place an existing art can be; the watchtower, the market, the square, the bee house, the church
and the houses' rooms are agent B's part, the new arts agent A's (scratchpad places.md, the round's brief).

The format and the rules are companion/SCENES.md's: "Scenes of a culture pack" (the new paragraph and rows), "Seasons" (a
row), "What the village has built" (which landmark leads where), "Arts" (the slots, spots and effects used).

### How the projects fit the arts they are drawn in

The square draws the maypole, the fountain and the tower's clock, the stream the cart bridge and the field the big barn
(SCENES.md "What the village has built": `SceneArt.projects`), each as scenery: there is no slot for them. So a project
scene's things are its place's slots (the square's twelve, the stream's fourteen, the field's twenty), every one a word,
and the project's own words (la fontana, il ponte, l'albero della cuccagna, il grande fienile, l'orologio …) are its pack's
and its dialogs', where its turns test them. The lines speak of the project as the art draws it: the greasy pole's wreath
and four ribbons (its prizes are told as hung for the feast: the square draws none yet), the fountain's four spouts and its
water (`fountain`), the bridge's piers and the cart over its deck (`cart`), the barn's eight posts and its roof, snow on
it in winter. Friuli's toplar is the big barn the field draws, as looks-friuli-out.md found.

The square's three Friuli scenes name its things with In piazza's words and show In piazza's I spy clues (agent B's, from
the Friuli piazza at every level of the linden and the well; the tower's with its clock in L'orologio), and the bee meadow
Dalle api's: the same picture, the same words and clues. A level of a building adds scenery, never takes a word away
(SCENES.md "Every building at its level"), so no line counts what a level changes: the hives are "the hives the children
painted", the linden is told climbed and lost under, not big; Nel campo's clue for the fienile "Ha due archi" (it has one
at the hayrack's first level) is "Ha un grande arco" now, in Il grande fienile's clues too.
