# Built: the Friuli village's projects to step into

Jan, 2026-10-30: "teo upgraded the tower - now he can't enter it > can you build all scenes for all upgrades including slo
culture?" Teo (a child of about seven learning Italian from German, the Friuli culture, a tablet) could step into his
tent, kitchen, field, smithy, school and mill, and nothing else his village had finished: of its thirteen village
projects only the mill had a scene. This part of the round (agent C, branch `places-it-projects`) writes the Friuli scenes
for the projects whose place an existing art can be; the watchtower, the market, the square, the bee house, the church
and the houses' rooms are agent B's part, the new arts agent A's (scratchpad places.md, the round's brief).

The format and the rules are companion/SCENES.md's: "Scenes of a culture pack" (the new paragraph and rows), "Seasons" (a
row), "What the village has built" (which landmark leads where), "Arts" (the slots, spots and effects used).

What each holds:

- **Nella vigna**: Matteo among his vines every morning (pruning in winter, the new leaves in spring), the grape harvest in
  September and October (a counting variant: the crates for the cellar), Giulia chasing the starlings off the ripe grapes
  (August and September) and her persimmons in the autumn (a tap turn: find the tree), Lorenzo waiting for the red tractor
  and its six crates, Nonno Bepi watching a summer storm from the wall (the bell against the hail, the seconds counted to
  the thunder). Every effect of the art: the bell, the tractor, the starlings.
- **L'albero della cuccagna**: the landmark is a place only while the pole stands (GAME.md "A landmark with a season": in
  May, or two weeks after it is finished), so its talk names no month. Davide and the soaped pole he slid down (the cat
  that climbs the linden better than the boys), Davide greasing it in the morning (the ribbons in the wind), Lorenzo
  dreaming of the prizes hung for the feast and counting the four ribbons, Tommaso's football by the pole (the ball lost
  under the bench: a tap turn), Nonna Rosa at the feast on weekends (the band, the polka) and with frico or gubana for the
  climbers on weekdays.
- **La fontana**: Bruno and the four iron spouts he forged (Lei to signor Bruno; a counting variant of the spouts; the
  spouts frozen on a winter morning until the sun), Zia Nives hearing the news at the fountain (her radio's bora; a bit
  deaf), Giulia cooling off in summer (la mano, le mani) and playing her article game from October to May, Lorenzo filling
  his grandpa's jug (a counting variant), Bruno washing the smithy off his hands in the evening as the bell rings.
- **Il ponte**: Franco watching a cart loaded with planks cross his bridge (a counting variant of the carts) and telling
  its numbers (six metres, two pillars; the footbridge found with a tap), Bruno checking it the morning after a night's
  rain (the stream in flood; snow on the planks in winter), Davide's flock drinking by it (Bianca last), the firefighters'
  drill with their pump once the fire station stands (May to September), Tommaso and the trout (the dragonfly found with a
  tap), Aldo in the evening (the bell, the bats).
- **Il grande fienile**: Franco and the hay under the barn's roof in summer (a storm passing), Franco counting the
  village's stores (a counting variant from its food), Davide taking hay for his sheep in the cold months and watching the
  bats come out at dusk, Lorenzo counting the barn's eight posts (Baio back in the shafts) and drawing the scarecrow a face
  (the crows back on its arm, then chased off), Matteo startling a hare out of the grass.
- **Il prato delle api**: Aldo at the hives the children painted (the smoker, the comb; the bees quiet in winter), a swarm
  round the linden in May and June, Giulia at the honey extractor (acacia honey in spring, chestnut in the autumn), Rosa
  on the bench by the meadow's flowers (yellow and white in spring, the linden in flower in June and July, the clover the
  children sowed), Tommaso proud of his painted hive (two things found with a tap; a sting in the summer). Every effect of
  the art: the swarm, the smoke, the honey.
- **L'orologio del campanile**: the clock shows the phone's own hour and is lit at night, so no turn asks the learner to
  read an hour off it, and every happening needs the church it is drawn in. Maestra Elena and the clock's two hands on
  school-day afternoons (the long one the minutes; one o'clock from where she says the hands are; being on time), Tommaso
  playing before school until the bell sends him off (alle otto; his ball lost under the bench), Bruno and the gears he
  forged (the key; twelve strokes at noon), Bepi on the days before the clock (the sun, the shadow), Nives and her radio
  that tells the same time, the clock lit like a little moon on the winter evenings of November to January.

Across them: every happening with two or three variants, the dialog of before last, its memory; each scene has something
on in every month; Nonno Bepi never in the evening (his story's), the children never in the evening; registers as the
cast has them (Lei and "signor" to Franco and Bruno, "maestra" and Lei to Elena, tu to the others); everything that agrees
with the learner a gender pair in every language, Slovene duals with a woman paired; faces where a line carries a feeling;
every "guarda" cued or naming a thing of its art, every visible action cued with the art's effects; 4 dialogs count
(SCENES.md "Numbers": the day's crates, jugs and carts, the village's food as the barn's stores) and 7 turns are tap
turns, in 6 dialogs. No wine for a child (Matteo offers grape juice).

### How the projects fit the arts they are drawn in

The square draws the maypole, the fountain and the tower's clock, the stream the cart bridge and the field the big barn
(SCENES.md "What the village has built": `SceneArt.projects`), each as scenery: there is no slot for them. So a project
scene's things are its place's slots (the square's twelve, the stream's fourteen, the field's twenty), every one a word,
and the project's own words (la fontana, il ponte, l'albero della cuccagna, il grande fienile, l'orologio …) are its pack's
and its dialogs', where its turns test them. The lines speak of the project as the art draws it: the greasy pole's wreath
and four ribbons (its prizes are told as hung for the feast: the square draws none yet), the fountain's four spouts and its
water (`fountain`), the bridge's piers and the cart over its deck (`cart`), the barn's eight posts and its roof, snow on
it in winter. Friuli's toplar is the big barn the field draws, as looks-friuli-out.md found.

The square's three Friuli scenes share its things' words and their I spy clues with the square scene of B's part to come
(the clues name no colour for the church, the tower and the houses, which Friuli's look repaints). The bee meadow's art is
the bee house: the meadow full of flowers, the hives the children painted (spoken of by their many colours, not the
Slovene paintings), the little bee house: as its project's steps say.

## The words

Seven new packs in `companion/cultures/friuli/packs/` (102 words, `it` with its article, `sl`, `en`, `de`, examples in all
four, notes in English, Slovene and German): `nella-vigna`, `la-cuccagna` (with the square's tiglio, panchina, pozzo and
bicicletta that no Italian pack had), `la-fontana`, `il-ponte`, `il-grande-fienile`, `il-prato-delle-api`, `l-orologio`.
Where an Italian pack had a thing's word, the scene names that pack (in-paese's vigna, chiesa, campanile, strada, ponte and
fontana; festa-vendemmia's vite and grappolo; Al ruscello's and Nel campo's every thing; famiglia's cane and gatto …), so a
word is one card wherever it is met. No two Italian words share an English, German or Slovene prompt (card.py's rule,
over 619 Italian words).

Words that agent B's packs may bring too, to keep as one card when both are on main: tiglio, panchina, pozzo, bicicletta
(the square), the bee house's words (ape, miele, arnia, favo, cera, affumicatore, smielatore), and the watchtower's
`sulla-torre` (already on B's branch): ringhiera, tetto, tuono, the same meaning as here (one card); `lampo`'s German is
"Lichtblitz" so it doesn't share "Blitz" with B's `fulmine`.
