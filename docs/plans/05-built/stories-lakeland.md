**The topics.** The brief named six: the Lambton-style worm tales of the north, the Giant of Mardale, the Keswick
pencil, Herdwick sheep and the shepherds' meets, Beatrix Potter's farms, the rushbearing. Five were settled against
sources as they are. **No "Giant of Mardale" was found**: the sources on Mardale (en.wikipedia "Mardale", Visit Eden's
"Myths and legends of Eden", lakelandwalkingtales.co.uk, the Old Cumbria Gazetteer) know the Holmes, "Kings of Mardale",
and the drowned village, and the region's only giants are Penrith's Giant's Grave (already Wilf's fourth story) and Sir
Tarquin of Lammerside, who "ate small boys". So the Mardale story is the Kings of Mardale and the village under
Haweswater. The worm tale is the Lambton Worm itself (County Durham, told from over the Pennines): Cumbria has no land
worm of its own in the sources (only Arnside's Hagg Worms, a line of hearsay).

## How

The stories follow `tom-fool-of-muncaster.json`'s format (SCENES.md "Stories"), in the compact style of Friuli's newer
files; three (the Herdwicks, Mrs Heelis, the rushbearing) were written by a helper on the same brief and checked here
like the others. A scratch lint stood in for the Android tests while writing: the bridge's `validateStory` with the
culture's packs and cast and the curated pictures' count, and DialogReactionsTest's and SceneCoherenceTest's rules
approximated (a reaction at most 80 characters said to a man and to a woman, no sky cue on it, never the right choice
nor the words that set it apart, in any language; no "look" line; two right choices with their own replies and the
same cues). The readings were checked with the bridge's `cultureReadings`.

## How to use

- The learner of English: by the fire in the evening, as before: once the ten older stories are heard, Old Wilf tells
  the new ones in their order (Home's "Tonight by the fire …" names tonight's). The notebook keeps them with their
  sketches. The corner ("📖 Reading") lists the four readings among the rest, by level; each waits until its reader
  lives in the village and was met (`Readings.knows`: Edna, Poppy and Granny Maggie, Shepherd Jack, Mr Tyson).
- QA: `LANI_DEV_DATA=<a synthetic learner of English who has heard the ten> LANI_CULTURE=lakeland
  LANI_QA_SETUP_PAIR=en-de LANI_QA_LEARNER=Ana LANI_QA_STORY_SHOTS=<dir> LANI_QA_READING=a-letter-from-keswick
  LANI_QA_READING_SHOTS=<dir> companion/bin/qa-slot --no-town --steps story-faces,culture-reading`. The synthetic
  learner: `bun test/fixtures/make-learner.ts <dir> --name Ana --target en --base de`, then in `<dir>/app/game.json`
  Old Wilf and Auntie Edna among the residents and met (`state.residents`, `state.bonds.<id>.met`: the fixture's six are
  the first six ids, without Wilf), and the ten older stories heard (`state.stories`: `{"told": 1, "level": "A1", "on":
  "<date>", "levels": {"A1": 1}}` each).
