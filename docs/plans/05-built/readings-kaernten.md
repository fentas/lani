# Kärnten's readings and Opa Sepp's stories to B2 (readings-kaernten, built 2026-11-03)

Wave E, batch 2b, section 2 (followups-16.md) for Kärnten, the German village in the lower Gailtal. Before this round
Kärnten's corner had readings at A1 4, A2 4 and B1 1, and Opa Sepp told his sixteen stories at A1 and A2 only: a learner
of German at B1 or B2 heard a retelling at A2 and found one reading at their level. Now the corner has three readings at
B1 and three at B2, and every one of the sixteen stories has a B1 and a B2 telling with its notes. Everything written
here is machine-written (Claude) and flagged so; the native-check list comes first.

## How

A compact authoring format (one line a spoken line, a choice, its reason or its reply, in de, sl, en and it) turned into
the stories' JSON in their house layout by a scratch script, which inserts the B1 and B2 levels after the A2 and the notes
after the A2's, and adds the review note. A scratch lint stood in for the Android tests while writing, as the earlier
round's did: `DialogReactionsTest`'s rules (a reaction at most 80 characters said to a man or a woman, never the right
choice nor the words that set it apart, `ChoiceDiff`'s longest-common-subsequence marks recomputed, in any language),
one or two right and a wrong one, the reasons in sl, en and it, the faces, the grammar ids, lines under 200 characters,
the paragraphs against the pictures' places, the notes' places and paragraphs; then `bun test/check-culture.ts kaernten`
on every story. The glosses went the same way: compact lines (lemma, part of speech, gender, the three meanings, the forms
with a short code for each grammar label) merged into the supplement, extending an entry the lemma already has.

## How to use

- A learner of German at B1 or B2 in the Carinthian village: by the fire in the evening, as before; Sepp now tells at
  their level. The notebook keeps each telling's notes with its sketches. The corner (»📖 Lesen«) lists the new readings
  by level; each waits until its reader lives in the village and has been met.
- QA: a synthetic learner, `bun test/fixtures/make-learner.ts <dir> --name Ana --target de --base en --level B1`, with
  Sepp, Stanko and Mitzi added to `state.residents` and every resident met (`state.bonds.<id>.met`) in `<dir>/app/game.json`,
  then `LANI_DEV_DATA=<dir> LANI_CULTURE=kaernten LANI_QA_SETUP_PAIR=de-en LANI_QA_LEARNER=Ana
  LANI_QA_STORY_SHOTS=<shots> LANI_QA_READING=die-kaerntner-biene LANI_QA_READING_SHOTS=<shots> companion/bin/qa-slot
  --no-town --steps story-faces,culture-reading`.
