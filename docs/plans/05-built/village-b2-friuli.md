# village-b2-friuli: Friuli's village to B2

Wave E, batch 2b, section 3 (followups-16.md), for Friuli (Italian). Base main 1311eeb2a.

## For a native check first (machine-written, Claude)

Everything below is machine-written: the Italian, and the Slovene, English and German beside it. Nothing was reviewed by a native
speaker. Worth a native eye first:

- **The charcoal pile's words.** *aia* for the pile's clearing (the craft's *aia carbonile*), *catasta* for the stacked pile, *ciocco*
  for a log, *fori* for the vent holes, *cuocere* and *la carbonaia cuoce* for the slow burn, *scarbonatura* (only a stage id, never
  shown), *gerla* for the back basket, *squarciarsi* for a pile that tears open. Slovene *kopišče*, German *Meilerplatz*, *Sode*.
- **Puns in reactions** (a reaction may not say the right form, so some are jokes): Marisa's «Serva? … una serva qui non ce l'ho»
  (B2 arrival, se Le servisse), Bepi's «Come un promesso sposo?» (essendo promesso), Franco's «Noi non siamo in vendita» (saremmo
  comprato), Bruno's «Ti sembro una signora?» (della quale). Check they read kindly, and for a child.
- **Wrong choices that must stay a learner's slip**, never a form people say (followups-12 "What people say"): the B2 tellings test
  auxiliaries (avrei venuto, sarebbe immaginato), agreement (va lasciato for le pietre, del quale for la pompa), fare/lasciare + a
  (ti faccio a vedere), se + present subjunctive (se possano), a conjunction with the future (purché insegnerai, prima che verrà),
  reported speech with the future perfect (sarà durata). I avoided the indicative for the subjunctive and se + conditional, which
  people do say. One existing B1 arrival (Giulia's «Penso che tu sei», not mine) offers a form adults say in speech as wrong: a
  finding for the lead, unchanged.
- **Facts added** (none contradicts VILLAGERS.md, the arrivals or the stories as far as I checked): Rosa raised a daughter; Davide's
  grandfather's words on patience; Bepi went to work "beyond the Alps"; Matteo's grandfather planted the vineyard; the school's desks
  are ordered in Gorizia. Pieri: forty years at the piles since he was fifteen, a wife at home, a daughter studying in Udine to be a
  teacher, a grandfather who spent twenty summers in Austria's woods; Bruno pays partly in nails and axes.
- **The lexicon table** `companion/lexicon/tools/it_cov_70_village_b2_friuli.txt` (131 lines): every meaning written by hand.

**Steps at B2.** Each of the 78 steps has a B2 telling beside its others: the same work in other words, 4–7 lines said by the
project's people, a turn testing a rule of B2 (with its puzzled reaction) and the step's words tested as at every level (a wrong choice
differing in the word, or its thing tapped). The taps, the fx cues and the `done` line sit where the B1 telling has them, so the picture
moves the same. A helper's lines say nothing of who says them; the steps name only their leader. Rules asked (18 B2 pages): the past
conditional's auxiliary, the irreal if, the pluperfect and past subjunctive, the past gerund and the past infinitive, the participle
alone, il quale, fare and lasciare with an infinitive, the passive with venire and andare, purché, benché, affinché, prima che, a meno
che, per quanto, come se, concordanza after vorrei/credevo/avrei voluto, reported speech in the past, connectives, the suffixes.

**Arrivals at B2.** Every villager's introduction and the newcomer's: the late line, 6–8 lines, two or three turns, each telling with
a B2 rule; the registers as before (Lei to Franco, Bruno, Marisa, Elena and an adult newcomer; tu and nonno/zia to the others).

Plus his meeting, three greetings, "missed", every stage's `done`, the breakout (on a white or yellow smoke night; the race 25, 18, 15
and 12 s at A1, A2, B1 and B2) with saved, lost and missed, two nights (the bora, a fox), Bruno's lines on his card (says, waits,
cold), and his own story in six parts (after 2, 5, 9, 14, 19 and 25 evenings). Every talk and piece at A1, A2, B1 and B2; the spot's
history (its card) at B1 and B2 too, naming Bruno as the A1 and A2 ones do; his meeting at B1 and B2 as `keeper.levels` too. A1 and A2
turns test the words and the Lei; B1 and B2 turns name their grammar pages (B1's and B2's).

What it changes in a Friuli village (as in Primorska's, generic code, nothing new): while a smithy stands, the felling and stacking
take the round's wood from the village's store once; the delivery puts "♨️ Il carbonaio Pieri ha portato il carbone al fabbro Bruno"
in the chronicle, Bruno speaks of it for two weeks, and his forge needs a quarter less wood for one forging.

**Sources** (as Primorska's: there is no Friulian record of the stages' days): the Friulian mountains' charcoal burning until the
1950s and the black clearings still along the beech wood's paths above Piancavallo
([turismofvg.it, Ring of the carbonaie and Col Cornier](https://www.turismofvg.it/en/active/ring-of-the-carbonaie-and-col-cornier));
the stages, the vent holes, the smoke from white to blue, earth never water on a breakout, and the yield of about a fifth of the wood
as the Alpine craft has them ([Museo etnografico di Premana, "Carbonèer, jaal e pojàt"](https://www.museo.premana.lc.it/2020/10/carboneer-jaal-pojat-ovvero-carbonai-carbonaie/));
Friulian seasonal emigration, many as woodcutters, to Austria and to Romania (general sources on the emigration, not on charcoal
burners: [Alpina friulana, "Passi degli emigranti"](https://www.alpinafriulana.it/passi-nella-storia-del-fvg/passi-degli-emigranti-passi-nella-storia-del-fvg/),
[Orizzonti culturali italo-romeni, the Friulians in Romania](https://www.orizzonticulturali.it/it_studi_Silvia-Biasutti.html)). The
Carnia, the Val Resia, Udine and Venice are the pack's own A2 history.
