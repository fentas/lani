# The Lake District's readings and stories to B2 (readings-lakeland, built 2026-11-03)

Wave E, batch 2b, section 2 (followups-16.md): the Lake District (English, Northern English as spoken in Cumbria) had
readings to B1 (one) and its sixteen stories at A1 and A2 only. This round writes readings at B1 and B2, three of each
now, and B1 and B2 tellings of Old Wilf's stories, from the Lakes' real legends, customs and history, with their sources.
Everything is machine-written (Claude), flagged as such, and needs a native check (first, below).

### Old Wilf's stories at B1 and B2 (`companion/cultures/lakeland/stories/`)

Each story keeps its A1 and A2 tellings, pictures and words and gains a B1 and a B2 telling (14–16 lines, four turns,
every wrong choice with its why in en, sl, it and de and Wilf's reaction, most naming a B1 or B2 page of the English
book) and the notebook's notes for both. A B1 telling narrates with the past continuous and perfect, used to, the
passive, relative clauses and the conditionals, and asks the learner to reason ("Why do you think…?", "What would you
have done?"); a B2 telling tells more of what the sources say, with participle clauses, inversion ("No sooner had they
begun", "Should you…"), the passive of report ("is thought to have fallen"), past modals, wishes and mixed
conditionals, and the learner's turns test those.

## How

The tellings were written in a compact source and built into each story file after its A2 telling and A2 notes, so
the older lines are untouched (scratch scripts, not committed: build, a lint of DialogReactionsTest's, NotebookTest's
and SceneCoherenceTest's rules, the 200-character limit, the pages named). `bun test/check-culture.ts lakeland`
validates each story with the packs and the cast.

Older apps: the stories' levels have reached C2 in the app since its first version (Stories.LEVELS), the readings' B2
too (Readings.LEVELS), so 0.2.698 reads the new tellings and readings as they are; no new field.

## How to use

- By the fire: a learner at B1 or B2 hears tonight's story at their level where it has it; a learner at A2 never
  hears more than A2 the first time, and a retelling goes a level up (Stories.tonight).
- The corner: the five readings among the rest, by level; each once its reader is met.
- QA: `bun test/fixtures/make-learner.ts <dir> --name Ana --target en --base de --level B1`, then in `<dir>/app/game.json`
  add `wilf` and `tyson` to `state.residents` and give them `state.bonds.<id>.met`; `LANI_DEV_DATA=<dir> LANI_CULTURE=lakeland
  LANI_QA_SETUP_PAIR=en-de LANI_QA_LEARNER=Ana LANI_QA_READING=the-night-the-bridge-went LANI_QA_READING_SHOTS=<shots>
  LANI_QA_STORY_SHOTS=<shots> companion/bin/qa-slot --no-town --steps story-faces,culture-reading`.
