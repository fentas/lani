*Built 2026-11-03, branch `course-it-b2-c`, from `outlines-2`. Jan, 2026-11-02: "yes complete". Italian's B2 was outlined as 18 units; three branches write six each. This branch writes the last six: other forms, words, a text. Everything here is machine-written (Claude) in Italian, German, Slovene and English, and waits for a native speaker's check.*

## For the native check

Every unit and page says it is machine-written (`review` names what most needs a look). What most needs an Italian speaker's eye, and a German and a Slovene one's for the bases:

- **infinito-passato:** il mangiare for food ("Da Marisa il mangiare è sempre buono"); the subject after the verb in Dopo essere tornati, Bruno e Aldo mangiano; Finita la colazione dei clienti; Vistolo arrivare on the page, for books only. German: the *nachdem* sentences. Slovene: the perfective present (Ko poje, se Bruno vrne).
- **Dopo mangiare and dopo mangiato:** neither is offered as wrong, as both may be heard. Typed, they are wrong. Someone should say whether either is a spoken form worth listing.
- **Finito i compiti:** typed, it is wrong (the ending). Offered in no choice or bank. Is it common enough to be a spoken form?
- **fare-lasciare:** Faccio leggere Lorenzo; fagliela vedere; lascia provare and fai provare as equals; Lascia lì! accepted beside Lascia stare!; the doubled pronouns as spoken forms (Gliela faccio vedere al nonno, te lo faccio vedere come si fa); Te lo posso fare vedere beside far vedere. Slovene: *dati* + the infinitive for fare (Franco da popraviti mlin, Lorenzu dam brati pismo), and Brunu pomaga Lorenzo for si fa aiutare. German: *Den lasse ich ihn lesen*.
- **viene-aperto:** è aperta against viene aperta at a set hour (both accepted in the gaps, and va as well); la piazza viene chiusa for closed to traffic; Si sono venduti tutti; La bici è in vendita as the meaning of Vendesi; the rule that andare + the participle in the sense of *must* goes only in simple tenses (è andata persa is another sense, left out).
- **il-quale:** chiunque or qualunque cosa + the indicative as spoken forms (arriva, succede). [Treccani's Enciclopedia dell'Italiano](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/aggettivi-e-pronomi-indefiniti_(Enciclopedia-dell'Italiano)/) gives chiunque voglia/vuole, and [the Crusca](https://accademiadellacrusca.it/it/consulenza/uso-del-congiuntivo/104) says speech often replaces the subjunctive with the indicative; neither says how common it is. Also: Chiunque as a spoken second answer for Chi arriva presto; col quale and coi quali accepted; Elena, il cui libro è nuovo; la quale in speech. Che il suo (for il cui) isn't called wrong anywhere, as it is popular speech. Slovene: katerega, katere for whose; Videm for Udine.
- **alterati:** the forms accepted: casina, casuccia, casettina, paesetto, cagnino, cagnolo, tavolinetto, micino; risatona and giornataccia as natural words; tempino, tempone, incontento and ricontento as wrong options; sregolare and slegale as "doesn't fit here"; follemente and mediocremente aren't taught, and the rule says a vowel before -le, -re. German: Lausbub, Mandelnougat. Slovene: paglavec also means tadpole, so the table says "poredni fant, paglavec", while the page's title keeps paglavec; "narediš ljubke" for the dear forms.
- **connettivi:** In base alle regole il mercato chiude alle due; Caro Franco + La as `almost`; per via, per colpa and a seguito accepted beside a causa; comunque, in più, e poi and naturalmente accepted in gaps; Il trattore Bruno l'ha riparato, Le sedie Marisa le porta; capitalised La, Le, Sua said to be "often"; Rispettosi and Tanti saluti accepted beside Cordiali and Distinti in a letter to the mayor. Gentile Sindaco for a mayor of any gender. German: Sehr geehrter Herr Bürgermeister assumes a man.
- **Benché + the indicative: spoken, or wrong?** connettivi's print says ✗ benché piove → ✓ anche se piove under "Attenzione". It stays for now, whatever the answer; a spoken form is never wrong, so the answer decides whether it may stay.
- **Offered as wrong across B2 and kept:** se + the conditional (se potrei, se avrei saputo: the outline and B1 say never) and disse che porterebbe. Is either said enough to come out? `nonstandard`, once it is in main, is the place for those that are, and for benché + the indicative, dassi and stassi typed.
- **The Slovene examples** are mostly masculine, as B1's are.
- **The supplement's new entries** (`it_cov_32_b2c.txt`, `it_cov_33_b2c.txt`, 100 entries in three bases), most of all the Slovene: ovčja staja, raznašalec pisem, dati si narediti, povrhu tega.

## How it was written

This branch wrote units 13–15 and gave 16–18 to a helper with the same brief (files only, no commits). A second reader (a fresh agent, read only, who saw the files and COURSE.md, not the reasoning) read all 303 items and the pages. Taken:

- **Right answers marked wrong:** Dopo che ha mangiato (the instruction now says dopo aver or dopo essere); "si è lasciata" and "ha fatto" where a gap had no cue (now "(fare)"); the verb first (Vanno lavati i bicchieri); the subject first (Marisa, arrivata a casa, si siede); the joins with il quale at the end (the instruction now says preposition + il quale); a bare tavolino where only "un tavolino" was listed, which the grading rejects by word count; portalettere, per colpa, oltretutto, Tanti saluti; La faccio vedere a Giulia, one pronoun where the list wants two (the instruction now says person and thing).
- **False or overstated explanations:** "same person" for grazie per aver aiutato (the one thanked helped); "with a thing, a" when the unit itself says da Bruno; "fare loses its -e" (often); "drei Bedeutungen" for viene fatto and si fa; "a small letter after the comma" right before capital Le; cui "only after a preposition" after a step on il cui; andare + the participle "simple tenses only" (in the sense of must); -le, -re losing their e (a vowel before them).
- **The right answer was always first:** all 75 choices had `answer: 0`, and the app shows options in the file's order. Each is rotated now, the answers at 19, 31 and 25 in the three places. Every course had the same, B1's 164 of 165 choices among them: `choices-order` (merged here) makes the app and the prints order a choice's options themselves, so nothing else was rotated, and these rotations now change nothing a learner sees.
- **Smaller:** a hint that spelt the distractor (-le + mente), a hint that named the answer (avere → ?), "warmen Teller" for un piatto caldo, Slovene "poljubiš" (kiss) for the dear forms, Mi piace il vino in a child's first person, bold sentences with no meaning after them (added).
