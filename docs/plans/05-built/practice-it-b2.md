Each has twelve exercises (choice with its options read aloud, multi, cloze with a hint, reorder with distractors, translate, speak
with `show: false`), each naming its page, every text the learner reads in `de`, `sl` and `en`, the learner's gender as `{m:…|f:…}`
where a form agrees with them. The quest is a request from its giver, stone 60 and wisdom 10, as B1's. Five of the givers had no
module before (Aldo, Matteo, Tommaso, Nives, Lorenzo).

**How the gaps were chosen.** A spoken form is never wrong (followups-10/12), and Italian speech often uses the indicative where
writing wants the subjunctive. So a gap uses a trigger without a spoken indicative (dubito che, volere che, prima che, senza che,
affinché, a patto che with voi, magari), or lists the spoken form in its `accept`; translations and speaking accept the spoken forms;
a wrong option is wrong Italian or wrong by its own sentence. Where tu's present indicative and subjunctive are the same form (tu
aiuti, tu ti lavi), the item asks another person.

**The pages.** Each B2 page now names the module with two or more of its exercises (`modules`), so its "🎯 Vadi · Practise" runs them
and playing the module unlocks it (GAME.md "The grammar book"); periodo-ipotetico names se-avessi beside condizionale-se, passato-remoto
racconta-come-ando beside trapassato-remoto.

**Telling a story** follows the page `raccontare`: the remoto for what is far, the prossimo for what is near and, in Friuli, nearly
always; the South has the remoto for yesterday too; none is wrong. So racconta-come-ando's gaps and its translation accept the prossimo
(dormimmo or abbiamo dormito), its multi has five right ways to tell, and a wrong option is only a wrong form or person (dissi for
il re, fondorono).

## Who meets it, and older apps

- **Modules:** `GET /modules` (the bridge, `ModuleStore.offered`) leaves out a curated module above the learner's level and paces the
  rest: one request open, at most one new a day, by level and `order`, its giver living here. So a B2 module reaches only a learner
  at B2, on any app: Teo's 0.2.698 gets the same list. 0.2.698 parses every exercise type these use, `almost` too (its
  `Models.kt` at a37c1765).
- **Drills:** the road offers a set, build or riddle at most one level above the learner's, and a set or build whose rule isn't
  introduced yet waits (`RoadDrills.offered`); the hunt takes riddles and builds at or below the level, transformations at it. Read
  at a37c1765 (0.2.698): the same three filters. So a child at A1 meets no B1 or B2 item; at A2 the B1 riddles (a child's world) and
  rapid sets of rules met; at B1, B2's riddles and the `secoli` set. And the road plays only what has its clips: none yet.
- `ItalianPracticeTest` holds this: every drill kind reaches B2, no B1 or B2 item is offered to a learner at A1, a B2 set waits for
  a B1 learner until its page is met.

**A finding for the lead (not changed here):** the pace offers the levels in order, so a learner who *starts* at B2 (or is placed
there by the coming placement test) gets A1's "Sono o ho?" first and B2's first module after the 21 below, one a day. Learners who
