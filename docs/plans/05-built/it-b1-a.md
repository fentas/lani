- **trapassato**: two past things, the one that came first (Quando Lorenzo è arrivato, Moro aveva già mangiato);
  avevo … avevano and ero … erano + the participle, its agreement (Giulia era già partita), the reflexive verbs (si era
  addormentata), già, prima, non … mai, perché, and what came first told second. German's Plusquamperfekt is the same
  tense (hatte gegessen, war abgefahren). The gap text: the evening of the festa; the test's: a Sunday at Nonna Rosa's.
- **passato-remoto**: the fairy tales' past, mostly to recognise, as the brief asks: -ò, -ì, -arono, -irono; fu, ebbe,
  disse, fece, venne (vide, prese, visse); the imperfetto's picture against what happens (un giorno, all'improvviso);
  the accent that counts (parlò, parlo; andò, andrò); German's Präteritum of tales against its spoken Perfekt. The gap
  text: Mina and the Agane of the stream; the list tells a tale as people talk (arrivò → è arrivato).
- **condizionale**: from vorrei to every verb (mangerei, giocherei, prenderei, dormirei), the future's short stems
  (sarei, avrei, andrei, farei, verrei, berrei), a wish, advice (dovresti, al posto tuo), imagining, asking kindly, and
  -emmo against the future's -emo. The gap text: the festa and its greasy pole.
- **congiuntivo**: after che and another person, -are → -i, -ere/-ire → -a, capisca; sia, abbia, vada, faccia, venga,
  possa, dica, stia; loro's -ino, -ano; the same person takes the infinitive (voglio giocare, penso di sì). The gap
  text: the day before the festa; the test's: a rainy day.
- **congiuntivo-espressioni**: è importante che, è meglio che, bisogna che; the feelings; prima che against prima di;
  perché for so that; senza che, basta che; dopo che, quando and so che keep the indicative. The gap text: the
  Epiphany bonfire (il pignarûl) and the bora.
- **periodo-ipotetico**: se + the present with the present, the future or the imperative; dreaming with se fossi, se
  avessi, -assi, -essi, -issi and the conditional (Se fossi un uccello, volerei sul Monte Sabotino); never «se sarei»;
  the se-half first or second. The gap text: the game «Se fossi…».

## Writing rules these units follow

- **No gap where another tense is Italian too.** Every trapassato gap is anchored before another past (quando + a
  past, già), so the spoken passato prossimo can't be a second answer there; a gap text a tense choice could answer
  two ways names the tense in its instruction ("Completa con il passato remoto", "… con il condizionale", "… con il
  congiuntivo") or in the cue ("(essere · condizionale)"), as A2's futuro did. A choice's wrong options are wrong by
  the sentence (un giorno, all'improvviso, altrimenti), and a narrative present is never offered as wrong (it is how a
  tale is told aloud).
- **A spoken form is never wrong.** After penso che and credo che the indicative (penso che è) is how many people
  talk: a choice offering it is `almost`, its why naming it as spoken and the subjunctive as the school's. Where speech
  commonly takes the indicative (after sperare che, the feelings, ho paura che, basta che), a gap or a transform line
  lists it in `spoken` (main's new key, 2026-11-01: graded right and labelled "Parlato, ed è giusto anche così; la
  forma standard: …"): 2 gaps of congiuntivo, 4 gaps and 5 lines of congiuntivo-espressioni. Two gaps after pensa che
  moved to vuole che, and a colloquial indicative is never a word bank's distractor (viene after è contenta che was).
- **passato-remoto is recognition first**: a sort by the verb a form comes from, choices of what a form means (vide: ha
  visto, not ha vinto, ha vissuto), a word bank, the tale retold in the passato prossimo; only the regular -ò, -ì and
  the five a child meets (fu, andò, disse, ebbe, venne) are written without a bank.
- **Several right answers listed**: both orders of a se-sentence, fa and farà after se domani, restò and rimase, the
  remote pasts that fit a gap without a verb in brackets, the subject named or left out; `whole: true` where a bracket
  is a frame (voglio che …), not the person.
- **What the grading couldn't tell apart is never typed**: voi's subjunctive and conditional are shown, never asked
  (until this branch's `endings`; the content stays so).

## The grading B1 needs

`grading/it.json` and the app's `Grading`/`Spelling` (data-driven, a table per language):

- **Another tense or mood one letter off is wrong, never a slip** (`wrong`, matched against the answer's word): the
  future's sarai, avrai, farai, darai for the conditional's sarei, avrei, farei, darei and the other way (five letters:
  the old count put the a/e in the stem); the indicative's sta for the subjunctive's stia; the remote past's fosti for
  the subjunctive's fossi and the other way; andò for the future's andrò. An accent forgotten stays almost (ando for
  andò, parlo for parlò: Italian's rule since A1).
- **The subjunctive's endings are endings** (`endings`, a new key of the table: regular expressions matched at the
  right word's end, longer than the plain one or two letters): -ano, -ino and -ono (dormono for dormano, vengono for
  vengano, siamo for siano: wrong; before, the vowel three from the end was in the stem and forgiven), -iate (parlate
  for parliate, siete for siate) and -este (the future's sarete, dovrete for the conditional's sareste, dovreste). A
  slip further in stays forgiven (venhano, capicano, magiate). siano for siamo stays a slip (m and n side by side, for
  whoever hasn't met the subjunctive). The longer ending applies to every Italian word that ends so (a typo in the vowel
  three from the end of bambino or piano is now wrong too), which the documentation says.
- `GradingLanguagesTest` pins both (two new tests). Documented where grading is: the README's two passages, COURSE.md's
  Italian row and B1 section, module-spec.md's matching rules, the fix skill (the tutor's guide topic "fix",
  regenerated with `tutor-export`), and the notes of `it.json` and `sl.json`.

- **condizionale**: «Al posto tuo, io metterei il cappotto» (mi metterei?); «Nonno, mi faresti guidare il trattore?»
  from a six-year-old; «Io ci salirei subito!» at the albero della cuccagna, the Slovene "mlaj za plezanje"; «Altrimenti
  io e Tommaso andremmo al mare»; the German würde-forms.
- **congiuntivo**: penso che è / sono as spoken (`almost`), voglio che + the indicative as wrong; Spero di vincere (sperare
  di isn't taught); «Voglio che Nonno Bepi sia felice»; the page's "the short forms come mostly from the present's io"
  (sia, abbia, sappia don't); the future after sperare excluded only by the instruction; Slovene "Upam, da si dobro".
- **congiuntivo-espressioni**: the indicative wrong after è meglio che and the feelings, where speech often has it;

  same subject) marked wrong; «Dopo che il fuoco è finito, tutti tornano»; German "Hauptsache" for basta che.
- **periodo-ipotetico**: the imperfect subjunctive in production for a child (fossi, avessi, giocassi, -ssimo, -ssero);
