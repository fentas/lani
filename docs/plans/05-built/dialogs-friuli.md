# Friuli's village at B1 and B2: the scenes' levelled variants

Wave E, batch 2a, `dialogs-friuli` (/mnt/scratch/github/fentas/.lani-lead-kit/followups-15.md, 2026-11-03), on the system of
`dialog-levels` (6e364137) and to its writers' guide (docs/plans/05-built/dialog-levels.md, as at 5fd43a08). The market,
`al-mercato`, is the model's and isn't touched here.

## Native check

Everything below is machine-written (Claude) and not reviewed by a native speaker: 128 dialogs, every line in Italian,
Slovene, English and German. Each scene's `review` line says so ("B1, B2 variants: machine-written, …"). For a native
speaker, first:

- **Italian, the lines Teo hears** (all 32 scenes; B1 for a child of seven, B2 for an older learner). Idioms and proverbs to
  confirm as natural in Friulian Italian: «Cielo a pecorelle, acqua a catinelle» (Franco), «il troppo stroppia» (Elena),
  «sbagliando s'impara», «la sai lunga» (Rosa), «stanco morto» (Elena), «dormi come un ghiro», «bagnato come un pulcino»
  (Davide), «mi si annoda la lingua» (Rosa), «non aveva tutti i torti» (Elena), «dare retta» (Bepi), «avere il fiatone» (the
  hundred steps). One saying is made up and given as a mother's: «Chi impara tardi impara meglio» (Rosa, polenta-b2). «Dì a
  Marisa…» (Bepi) is written with the accent; «Di' a Marisa» is the usual spelling.
- **Italian, the wrong choices.** Each must be a learner's mistake that no grown-up in Friuli says: se + the conditional
  («se avrei», «se sarebbe», «se ci arriverei», «se mi insegneresti»), the wrong auxiliary («avrei venuto», «avreste
  arrivati», «avrà rimasto», «aveva già tornata», «sarebbero camminate», «essendo ascoltato», «dopo essere controllato»),
  clitics not joined («mi lo», «gli lo», «le la», «ti li», «ti ne», «mi ne vado», «si ne va», «ce lo faccio»), verb + the
  wrong preposition («provo di», «insegniamo di», «comincio di», «smesso a», «riuscire di», «ci dimentichiamo a», «aiuto
  di»), relatives («in che», «con che», «da cui» for di cui, «in cui» for su cui), «ogni giocatori», «qualche pesci»,
  «ognuna ne prendono», «qualcune», «ognuni», agreement («è stata portata» for il ponte, «chiusa il portone», «sentito la
  notizia», «arrivato in cima» for two women), the short conditional regularised («anderei», «beverei», «averei»,
  «venirei», «sterebbe», «serverebbero»), the indicative after benché, sebbene, purché, affinché, a condizione che, a meno
  che («benché ruba», «purché li tiene», «a condizione che è»), «disse che verrebbe» (the course's page says never
  tornerebbe), «pensava che allontanino», «vorrei che suonerebbero», «stare per + gerund or participle». Never offered as
  wrong, as followups-12.md asks: the indicative after penso che, the imperfetto in a hypothetical («se lo sapevo,
  venivo»), «vorrei che venga», the passato prossimo for the remoto, «dove» for in cui, «ci vuole due ore».
- **Slovene translations** (Jan's wife): the learner's forms as gender pairs ({m:…|f:…}); the dual of the learner with a
  villager («sva {m:mešala|f:mešali}»); the vi of the learner to Bruno, Elena, Franco and Marisa (Lei in Italian);
  «ovčasto nebo, dež bo kot iz škafa» for the proverb; «suho kot seno avgusta»; «razkrinkal si me».
- **German** (with du, as Friuli's content has it): «Schäfchenhimmel, Regen wie aus Kübeln», «Müllerehrenwort», «zu viel ist
  ungesund» for il troppo stroppia.
- **Facts the lines tell**, checked: World Bee Day on 20 May, Anton Janša's birthday (born 1734 in Breznica), proposed by
  Slovenia ([FAO, World Bee Day](https://www.fao.org/world-bee-day/en)); Janša the first appointed teacher of apiculture
  for the Austrian lands in 1770 ([Wikipedia, Anton Janša](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Jan%C5%A1a)); the 3rd of April,
  the Patria del Friuli of 1077, «Fieste de Patrie dal Friûl» by a regional law of 2015, the golden eagle on blue
  ([Wikipedia, Bandiera del Friuli](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandiera_del_Friuli),
  [Patria del Friuli](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patria_del_Friuli)). Taken from the cast and earlier content, not
  checked again: Castelmonte near Cividale, the casoni of the lagoon, Manzano's chairs, the villotta, the gubana, the bells
  rung against hail. Common knowledge, not sourced: maize from America, polenta of buckwheat or millet before it.

## What

Each of the 32 scenes but the market got two B1 and two B2 variants, as the guide asks: one of each on two grown-ups'
happenings, so a learner at either level meets one of their own at each. Where a scene has one grown-up's happening for
the village's own learner (al-fuoco's `tisana`, il-mare's `vela`, in-soffitta's `culla`), all four are on it. The
storyteller's happening, the children's games and I spy are as they were; no happening has a level of its own.

**128 dialogs** (64 B1, 64 B2), **751 lines**, **257 turns**, **514 choices**; every turn has one right and one or two wrong
choices; 204 of the 257 wrong ones name a grammar page, the rest are about meaning (a B2 turn's "the reply that fits", as the
guide says). B1: 5–7 lines, two turns; B2: 6–8 lines, two turns, three in mandi-b2-lingue. Each with a memory of its own,
first in its happening's `dialogs` (B2, B1, then the old ones), the happening's `dialog` and old variants untouched.

The B1 pages named (times): condizionale 15, pronomi-combinati 14, discorso-indiretto 11, infinito-preposizioni 10,
relativi-cui 10, futuro-anteriore 8, indefiniti 7, congiunzioni 7, periodo-ipotetico 6, passivo 6, trapassato 5,
verbi-pronominali 5, stare-per 4, gerundio 4, si-impersonale 4, congiuntivo-espressioni 2, congiuntivo 1, passato-remoto 1
(and A2's futuro once). B2: congiunzioni-congiuntivo 11, periodo-ipotetico-irrealta 10, connettivi 6, fare-lasciare 5,
discorso-indiretto-passato 5, condizionale-passato 4, congiuntivo-trapassato 4, congiuntivo-imperfetto 4,
congiuntivo-indipendente 3, participio-assoluto 3, relativi-il-quale 3, gerundio-passato 3, passivo-venire-andare 2,
passato-remoto 2, congiuntivo-passato 2, alterati 2, infinito-passato 2, raccontare 2, concordanza-tempi 1 (and B1 pages
where a B2 line needs them). Every page is at most one level above its dialog (`levelPageErrors`).

**How the turns were written.** The congiuntivo after penso che is never tested against the indicative (people say «penso
che è»): its turns put the congiuntivo in the right choice and make the wrong one a mistake nobody makes (the auxiliary,
che + an infinitive, the conditional after vorrei che). A question never gives its answer's form away: Rosa doesn't ask
«me lo insegni?» before the learner says it; where a first draft did, the line was changed. The learner says Lei to
Bruno, Elena, Franco and Marisa, tu to the others (VILLAGERS.md), with vi in Slovene and Sie in German. The happening's
person answers every turn; no one else speaks. Nothing is offered to drink (Matteo's barrel is filled with grape juice only
in a question), no money worries, no romance, no wolves. What the lines say of the village keeps to the game: the bridge,
the clock, the fire station, the mill and the linden are its projects, so the old ones are told as gone (the wooden bridge
a flood took, the linden the bora broke, the old mill down by the river) and the new ones as the village's.

**Weather.** The four that bring it do as their happenings' own dialogs: Davide's rain in the tent and Bepi's two storms
over the vineyard (sky cues, `sky_stays: false`, Davide back to the door at the end), Davide's fog in the wood (fog in, the
sheep bells, fog out).
