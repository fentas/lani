How they keep to the course:

- **Only earlier grammar around a gap**: the six cases in the singular and the adjectives through them come before
  (the case units); `cigav` keeps `svoj` (B1) out: no sentence where the owner is the subject; `vezniki` comes last, so
  no earlier unit uses zato, čeprav or kadar.
- **Never a natural form graded wrong**: a sentence may start with a short word ("Se gremo kopat.", "Sem mu jo dal.",
  "Ji ga bom kupil." are accepted); after moram, hočem the short infinitive of speech (*moram delat*) is accepted beside
  delati; ko, kadar and če are all right where each fits; ampak beside vendar; od + genitive beside kot. Where both
  aspects would be right, the sentence was changed until its words decide (zdaj, ves dopoldan, v petih minutah, nehaj,
  a whole action then sent); "Jutri kupim" is the one left for the native check.
- **The learner's gender**: every first-person l-form a pair (`{m:pil|f:pila}`), in the explanations too; accepted in
  both genders where the speaker's is unknown (Daj mi kruh! → Bi mi dal/dala kruh?).
- **Names the forms** where the word is a pack's card (`lemma` + `form`): the plural cases of konj, panj, krava, hiša, sosed,
  travnik, okno, otrok, jabolko, mačka, prijateljica, gost; the comparative (`cmp.m.sg`) of star, visok, sladek, drag,
  majhen, hiter.

## What the units turn on

COURSE.md's table "What a unit turns on" has an A2 part: `primernik` asks an adjective's comparative on the forms
cards (`cmp.m.sg`); `mnozina-skloni` adds no row: a case's plural (hišah, hiš, hišam, hišami) comes with that case's page
and `mnozina` (as `mestnik`'s did at A1), so the case units before it open them; `cigav` as `spol`. The others turn on
no form of a table.

For the lead: if the plural rows of the genitive, dative and instrumental should wait for `mnozina-skloni` (the unit that
teaches them) instead of opening with each case unit, `pageOf` in bridge/src/forms.ts would name `mnozina-skloni` for an
oblique plural; not changed here (it is the A1 design, and the case units' rows depend on it).

## For the native check

Every unit's `review` names what to look at; in short:

- `mnozina-skloni`: the gap text "Sobota pri Vidi"; "Kovač Tone danes podkuje konje".
- `zaimki-skloni`: "Potica za vse" (njemu … njej side by side); "Nejc ji jo je vzel"; "Ji ga bom kupil" accepted
  (clitic-first); njiju; "Mi jo daš?".
- `cigav`: "Nered pri Micki"; Tonetov, Jožetov (a name in -e grows a t, as in its cases), Nejčev, babičin; njun.
- `primernik`: "Tek pod lipo" (najglasneje); kot or od; slabše as the adverb.
- `namenilnik`: the supines past, peč, teč, prinest (the spoken pečt, tečt accepted); "kupit kruh" or the older "kupit
  kruha" (both accepted); the full infinitive after a verb of going (*grem spati*) is graded wrong, as the norm has it:
  should it be almost?
- `glagolski-vid`: each gap of "Nedelja pri Micki"; "Jutri kupim Zali nov zvezek" (kupujem marked wrong); "Takoj pridem".
- `pogojnik`: "Gost na terasi" (Kaj bi pili? Bi vam prinesel še kruha?); "Zala bi rada psa"; krilo for wing; the
  children saying vi to babica, as A1 has it.
- `vezniki`: "Lukov dan" (ko for one evening, kadar or ko or če for every time); "Pridem jutri oziroma pojutrišnjem"
  (no comma, as the dictionary's examples have it).

Looked up in Fran (SSKJ², Slovenski pravopis): gost (gostje), otrok (pet otrok, z otroki), človek (ljudi, ljudmi),
podkovati (perfective), tih (tišji), lahek (lažji), sladek (slajši, also sladkejši: so not a wrong option), pasti
(pasem), speči (spečem, spekel), ujeti, gobec, oziroma (a correction of what was said), sankati se.
