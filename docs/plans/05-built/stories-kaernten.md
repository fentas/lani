# Opa Sepp's stories and Kärnten's readings (stories-kaernten, built 2026-11-01)

Jan, 2026-11-01: "make the app complete". Kärnten (the German village in the lower Gailtal below the Dobratsch, for
future learners of German from Slovene, English and Italian, adults) had 10 evening stories and 4 readings; Primorska has
16 and 9. This round writes 6 more stories and 5 more readings, so Kärnten has as many, with a pack for the stories'
words, and every word the new German lines use glossed in the German supplement, so a tap on it means it. Everything
written here is machine-written and needs a native check (the list at the end).

## What

### Opa Sepp's six new evening stories (`companion/cultures/kaernten/stories/`)

Each is told in one evening at A1 (the present, very short sentences) and A2 (the past, more detail), as the ten
before: 14–16 lines a telling, 4 turns for the learner, every wrong choice with its why (sl, en, it) and Sepp's reaction
(taking the slip at face value, under 80 characters), about half of them a form slip naming a German grammar page; 2–3
pictures from the vignette library and the notebook's notes for both tellings, all in de, sl, en and it. Adults' tales,
nothing gruesome, Austrian standard (Jänner, heuer, Bub, Mädel, Jause, Schlagobers, Flascherl).

### Every German word of them, glossed (`companion/lexicon/de.extra.json`)

German has no Wiktionary dictionary in Lani: a tap on a German word means it only when the supplement or a pack knows it,
and before this round only the course's 223 entries did. The new stories and readings used about 890 word forms none
knew. 657 new entries and 66 extended ones (the past, participle and imperative forms of sein, haben, geben, bringen,
fallen, werfen …) now mean every one of them in English (`gloss`), Slovene and Italian (`meanings`): the everyday words
(mit, man, nur, zum, über, war, ging), the verbs with their forms, the nouns with their plurals and datives, the places
and names (Dobratsch, Karawanken, Myra, Maria Saal, Bleiberg, Hochstetter), the Austrian words (Schlagobers, Dotter,
Sauerrahm, Mädel, Bub, Flascherl, Vogerl) and the Slovene words the tales quote (štehvanje, prvi rej, čisava župa, škrat,
knap). Left out: sounds (Hm, Psst, miau, wau, hov) and the wrong forms the turns offer on purpose (Nusse, getragt,
ausgetrinkt, Pfahle). The supplement has 880 entries.

## How

The stories follow `die-heilige-hemma.json`'s format (SCENES.md "Stories"). A scratch lint stood in for the Android
tests while writing: the reactions' rules of `DialogReactionsTest` (80 characters said to a man or a woman, no sky cue,
never the right choice or the words that set it apart, in any language: `ChoiceDiff`'s marks recomputed), the turns (one
or two right, at most three), the grammar ids, the bridge schema's lengths (`about` 400, a line 200, a caption 160, a note
600), `sky_stays` only with a sky cue, the pictures' paragraphs and the notes' places; a second script checked the new
words' meanings against every German pack, a third listed the words a tap couldn't mean.

## How to use

- A learner of German in the Carinthian village: by the fire in the evening, as before: once the ten older stories are
  heard, Opa Sepp tells the new ones in their order (Home's "Heute Abend am Feuer …" names tonight's). The notebook keeps
  them with their sketches. The corner ("📖 Lesen") lists the five readings among the rest, by level; as every reading, one
  read by someone of the cast waits until they live in the village and the learner has met them (`Readings.knows`:
  Stefan's letter, Kathi's lake, Franz's tower, Resi's soup, Florian's mountain).
- QA: `LANI_DEV_DATA=<a synthetic learner of German who has heard the ten> LANI_CULTURE=kaernten LANI_QA_SETUP_PAIR=de-en
  LANI_QA_LEARNER=Ana LANI_QA_STORY_SHOTS=<dir> LANI_QA_READING=der-woerthersee LANI_QA_READING_SHOTS=<dir>
  companion/bin/qa-slot --no-town --steps story-faces,culture-reading`. The learner: `bun test/fixtures/make-learner.ts
  <dir> --name Ana --target de --base en`, then in `<dir>/app/game.json` the ten older stories marked heard in
  `state.stories` (`{"told": 1, "level": "A1", "on": "<date>", "levels": {"A1": 1}}` each), Opa Sepp among the residents
  and Kathi and Sepp met (`state.bonds.<id>.met`).
