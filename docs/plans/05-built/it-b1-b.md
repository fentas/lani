# Built: the Italian school's B1 units 7–12 (2026-11-01)

Teo (a child of about seven, learning Italian from German in the Friuli village; a tablet) has A1 and A2 written, and B1
was outlined in `companion/course/it/course.json` with 18 units, every one planned: after A2, Maestra Elena only said
she was preparing the next lessons. Three branches write B1 side by side, in the outline's order: `it-b1-a` units 1–6
(trapassato to periodo-ipotetico), this branch (`it-b1-b`) units 7–12, `it-b1-c` units 13–18. The format and how a unit
is written are companion/COURSE.md ("Writing a unit", "The Italian course", and "The Italian course: B1, units 7–12" for
these); the A2 parts' docs (course-it-a2a.md, -a2b.md, -a2c.md) were the models.

Each is the size of A2's units: a guided intro of five or six steps (Maestra Elena's lines in German first with du,
then Slovene and English; every Italian form in bold for her voice with its meaning in brackets after it, German in
italics; two examples a step; the table growing row by row; a quick question each, choices and gaps), three worksheets
from easy to harder (the forms in a table or a sort with choices, then a gap text with a word bank, then transform
lists), and a test at pass 80 with new sentences and no bank.

- **pronomi-combinati**: the person first, then lo, la, li, le or ne, and the i of mi, ti, ci, vi turning into e (Me lo
  dai? Sì, te lo do.); ce la, ve li, me le; glielo, gliela, glieli, gliele in one word for him and her alike, and for
  Maestra Elena with a capital (Glielo porto, Maestra); the past (Chi te l'ha detto? Me l'ha detto Giulia.; La palla?
  Gliel'ho data.: the participle agreeing); on the end of an infinitive and an order (Puoi darmelo? or Me lo puoi dare?;
  Portagliela!; dammelo with da' doubling); me ne, gliene, and non before both. The gap text: the children's secret
  present for Zia Nives's birthday, a new radio (she can't hear well: «Come? Me lo ripeti?»). The test's: the school
  party.
- **si-impersonale**: si + the lui form as German *man* (In Friuli si mangia il frico; Come si dice «ciao» in friulano?
  Si dice «mandi».); the verb plural with a plural thing (si mangiano le castagne, al mercato si vendono le ciliegie);
  what is allowed (Si può entrare, Maestra? Non si tocca!); how something is done, step by step (Come si fa il frico?
  Prima si tagliano le patate, poi si mette il formaggio.); ci si alza, ci si diverte for a reflexive verb. The gap text:
  Nonna Rosa's frico, for Giulia; the test's: Maestra Elena's class rules.
- **gerundio**: the gerund alone at the front for while and when, the same person (Camminando, ho visto un riccio); by doing, how (Giocando si impara, and the proverb Sbagliando s'impara with unit 8's si); facendo, dicendo, bevendo, as with stare; the short words on its end, in one word (guardandolo, lavandosi, dicendogli); only for the person the sentence is about (Camminando, Giulia canta), quando or mentre for another (Mentre il riccio mangiava, Lorenzo stava zitto). The gap text: a walk in the wood with signor Franco and Lorenzo, and a hedgehog; the test's: a day at the sea.
- **stare-per**: stare per + the infinitive for what is about to happen (Sto per partire! Il treno sta per partire.); appena between avere or essere and the participle for what has just happened (Lorenzo ha appena mangiato, Giulia è appena arrivata); one thing at three moments (sta per piovere, sta piovendo, è appena piovuto or ha appena piovuto, both taught as right); stava per … quando for what someone was about to do when something happened; appena in front for as soon as (Appena arriva il treno, saliamo.), against ho appena. The gap text: the train to Trieste with the bora coming (Zia Nives's radio says so); the test's: the village festa.
- **relativi-cui**: cui instead of che after a preposition (la casa in cui abito, il bambino con cui gioco); cui never changing, the preposition saying what it means (a cui, di cui, da cui, per cui, su cui); che without a preposition and cui with one (il libro che leggo, il libro di cui parlo; chi only the question); dove for a place beside in cui (la casa dove abito), in cui for a time (dove there is spoken: a choice's almost); either order after cui (la panchina su cui si siede Nonno Bepi, su cui Nonno Bepi si siede). The gap text: Giulia's village; the test's: at the sea.
- **indefiniti**: qualcuno and qualcosa, and their no-answers nessuno and niente (C'è qualcuno? No, non c'è nessuno.); ogni + a noun in the singular (ogni giorno, never ogni bambini) and ognuno, ognuna with the verb singular (Ognuno porta qualcosa.); tutto, tutti with the article (tutti i bambini, tutto il giorno, tutta la notte) and tutti alone for everyone; qualche + the singular (A2's) against alcuni, alcune + the plural; qualcosa di buono, niente di nuovo. The gap text: the village festa where everyone brings something (Nonna Rosa the gubana, Aldo the honey); the test's: an afternoon in the village.

They assume A1, A2 and the B1 units before them in the outline. The first six B1 units (the trapassato, the passato
remoto, the conditional, the subjunctive, the if-clauses) are written beside these, so around a gap these units use
none of their forms; si-impersonale's Sbagliando s'impara comes after it, as gerundio's proverb.

## The grammar pages

Six new B1 pages of the Italian book (`companion/grammar/it/`), each its unit's summary card and first cheat-sheet card,
in it, sl, en and de, the book's usual size (a rule of about 1,000 to 1,200 characters per base, a table of 6 to 10 rows,

`relativi-cui` (the "later" that relative-che's page promises for cui), `indefiniti`. Each English rule says its
mistakes as "(never *…*)" for the print's "Achtung". The outline's six entries name their pages and are no longer
planned.

## The grading B1 needs

`grading/it.json` (the app's `Grading`, data-driven; `GradingLanguagesTest`):

- **The combined pronouns' mistakes are wrong, never a slip in the stem** (`wrong`): glilo, portaglilo for glielo,
  portaglielo (glie-'s e left out) and dammilo, dimmila, portamilo, darmilo, prestatilo for dammelo … (the i kept before lo,
  la, ne on the end of a verb). Before, each was one letter off, half an item for the very thing the unit teaches. mi lo,
  ti la and gliela for glielo were wrong already, by their ending; gli lo by its words. duemila, facile and the like are
  untouched (the patterns ask for a verb's shape).
- **glielo or gliela before avere not elided** is almost only where the participle agrees, as lo and la already were
  (glielo ho dato for gliel'ho dato, gliela ho data: almost, the apostrophe hint; glielo ho data, gliela ho dato: wrong).
- **The gerund with the other verbs' ending** is wrong (leggando, dormando, bevando, spingando for leggendo …; parlendo,
  camminendo, avvicinendosi, giochendo for parlando …), and so are fare's, dire's and bere's regular stems (farendo,
  direndo, berendo) and li for gli on its end (dicendoli for dicendogli). A slip in another letter stays forgiven
  (legendo, gaurdandoli), and dicendoli where it is asked is right.
- **Accents and apostrophes stay almost** as before (me l'ha for me lo ha, la sera, Sbagliando s'impara).
- Documented where grading is described: the README's two passages, COURSE.md, module-spec.md's matching rules, the fix
  skill and the tutor's guide topic made from it (`tutor-export`).

## Writing rules these units follow

- **Every right answer listed**: both places of a pronoun with a modal (Me la puoi prestare? Puoi prestarmela?); the
  orders Italian allows (Si mangia il frico, Il frico si mangia; In autunno si raccolgono le castagne and three more; su
  cui si siede Nonno Bepi, su cui Nonno Bepi si siede); è appena piovuto and ha appena piovuto; dove and in cui for a
  place, da dove for da cui; me li and ce li from the teacher; the subject named or left out (Tommaso ce la presta. Ce la
  presta.).
- **No trick**: no option is right Italian in its own sentence. ci si sta bene and ci si fanno i compiti are, so they
  were taken out of si-impersonale's choices (sta si, fanno si instead); dove for a time is spoken Italian, so it is a
  choice's `almost`, not its wrong option. A choice's `almost` is otherwise a right form in the wrong register: Te lo
  porto to Maestra Elena or to signor Franco, to whom the child says Lei.
- **Distractors that make the unit's mistake on purpose**: mi la, gli lo; mangiano, mettono (the verb's number against
  its thing); the -ando/-endo mix-ups (the grading makes each wrong for every gap, not only for its own).
- **Read aloud**: every bold form of an explanation is one the intro shows (its examples, its table, its questions or
  the unit's `words`: glieli, gliele, gliene are among pronomi-combinati's), so Elena reads it and never the phone's
  German voice (a check of this branch, below, found posso, sul, Tutti ridono and alcuni fichi first); only gerundio's
  endings -ando, -endo are, as in A2. German in an explanation is in italics; the smoke's course-speech check for Friuli
  passes.
- **The Slovene** puts its short words after the verb in a statement (Dam mu jo., Prinesem vam ga jutri.), a question's
  first (Mi ga posodiš?), and a first person's past pairs the learner's gender ({m:Dal|f:Dala} sem mu jo).
- **Nothing Slovene-only**: no `lemma`/`form`, nothing turned on.

## How to use

Nothing to switch on. A Friuli village's bridge serves the course from its checkout once this is merged (GET /course
reads the files per request), and the app's next build bundles it. The units come into Teo's school as B1 opens (the
school's levels 2 and 3 and A2 done), each the current one in turn; until B1's other twelve are written the school folds
them as "📝 In arrivo", and Maestra Elena says she is preparing the next lessons once the written ones are done.

```
# the prints, as Teo gets them (German base)
companion/bin/school-prints --language it --level B1 --base de --png --learner Teo --gender m
# QA: a synthetic Teo, a B1 unit end to end and its print
bun companion/bridge/test/fixtures/make-learner.ts <dir> --name Teo --target it --base de
LANI_DEV_DATA=<dir> LANI_ENV_FILE=/dev/null LANI_CULTURE=friuli LANI_CHILD=1 LANI_QA_SETUP_PAIR=it-de LANI_QA_LEARNER=Teo \
LANI_QA_IT_UNIT=pronomi-combinati LANI_QA_FRIULI_SHOTS=<shots> companion/bin/qa-slot --no-town --steps friuli-school-unit
```
