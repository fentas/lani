# The Lake District's talks at B1 and B2 (talks-en)

Built 2026-11-03 and 04 on branch `talks-en` (followups-16.md, section 1, English), from `talks-sl`'s tip (7b009be99),
with `talks-de` (which carries talks-sl, talks-it and packs-en) and main merged in at the end. The
free talks ("🗣️ Talk": role-plays where the tutor plays someone in a real situation) of the Lake District village were
eight, A1 and A2 (docs/plans/05-built/scenarios-de-en.md). Now there are six more at B1 and six at B2, for a grown-up, set
in the dale and in Keswick, with every text the learner reads in Slovene, German and Italian, the village's packs, and
the tutor's brief. They are offered by level as talks-sl built it: an A1 learner never meets a B1 or B2 one.
Everything written here is machine-written and needs a native check (the list comes first).

## Native check

Machine-written, every new file flagged in its `review` ("en, sl, de, it: machine-written, not reviewed by a native
speaker"). Facts and words settled with a source, one request at a time, with the URL; what a source doesn't settle is
listed for a native eye.

**Settled (the source says it as the talk uses it):**

- A faulty item can be rejected for a full refund within 30 days (the short-term right to reject, Consumer Rights Act
  2015, s. 22): https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/section/22. So the jacket's hint says "It's not a month
  old, so I think I can have a refund", and the assistant offers a repair and a voucher first.
- A tenancy deposit may be at most five weeks' rent where the year's rent is under £50,000 (Tenant Fees Act 2019,
  Schedule 1, para. 2): https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2019/4/schedule/1/paragraph/2. The owner asks six weeks;
  the hint "As far as I know, the deposit can't be more than five weeks' rent" answers him.
- Since 1 May 2026 a new tenancy in England has no fixed term (Renters' Rights Act 2025):
  https://clarkslegal.com/insights/articles/renters-rights-act-coming-into-force-on-1-may-2026/. So the cottage talk
  never offers "a year's let" or "sign for two years": the learner offers to "happily stay for years".
- The Senior Railcard takes a third off most rail fares from 60: https://www.railcard.co.uk/senior (and the operators'
  pages). The X4 and X5 run between Keswick and Penrith station, where Avanti's trains go to London Euston:
  https://bustimes.org/services/x4-workington-bus-station-w-keswick-bus-station-2. Maggie's hints name only "a bus from
  Keswick to the station", Penrith and Euston, no times.
- A dog near livestock should be on a lead (on Open Access land it must be): the Countryside Code,
  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-countryside-code/the-countryside-code-advice-for-countryside-visitors.
- A councillor's or an MP's "surgery" is their time to meet constituents one to one:
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_surgery. The setting and the role say "surgery"; the title says "A word with
  the councillor", so it isn't read as the GP's.
- Cumbrian: *nowt*, *owt*, *aye*, *bait* (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cumbrian_dialect). *Summat* (Dixon's and
  Jack's openers) isn't on that page: common across the North, to confirm for Cumbria. *We was* (Edna's opener) is what
  the grading table already treats as the North's and Cumbria's (`nonstandard`, grading/en.json: "We was late: the
  North's, Cumbria's", course-en-a2).
- Slovene base, on fran.si: *nepremočljiv* (SSKJ² "jopič iz nepremočljivega blaga"):
  https://fran.si/iskanje?View=1&Query=nepremo%C4%8Dljiv; *povodec* (SSKJ² "na povodcu je … vodil velikega volčjaka"):
  https://fran.si/iskanje?View=1&Query=povodec; *karavan* for an estate car (SSKJ², sense 1):
  https://fran.si/iskanje?View=1&Query=karavan; *serviser* (SSKJ², Pravopis), the garage's man:
  https://fran.si/iskanje?View=1&Query=serviser; *jagnjitev* is only in the livestock terminology (Živinoreja), not in
  SSKJ² or Pravopis: https://fran.si/iskanje?View=1&Query=jagnjitev. From talks-sl's list, as settled there: *varščina*,
  *priti komu naproti*, *krvni pritisk*, *razpotje*, *splošna ambulanta*.

All eight situations of the brief are there: a complaint, two negotiations, an appointment that goes wrong, the mayor (a
village in the Lakes has a parish and a district councillor, no mayor), a job at each level, a doctor's history, advice at
each level, what happened at each level.

**What a B1 or B2 talk does that an A1 or A2 one doesn't** (as talks-sl's): B1 has something that happened (the past:
"I bought it here two weeks ago", "At about midnight the water came into my kitchen"), a problem to explain and a wish
to argue for ("I'd rather have my money back", "Would you do it for two hundred and fifty?"), advice ("If I were you,
I'd …", "Why don't you …?"), the character pushing back once. B2 has an argument with reasons and concessions ("I
appreciate money is tight, but this is about safety"), a sequence told precisely and what one is sure of ("I'm fairly sure
about the jacket, but not about the car"), reported speech ("He said the dog had never done anything like that before"),
hypotheses ("What if you went after the lambing?"), a formal register kept (the councillor, the interviewer, the nurse,
the officer), and a negotiation that moves in steps and ends in a summary ("So: nine hundred a month, and the windows done
by October?").

**The cast plays its own.** Five talks name a villager of the cast, who plays them by name (their sprite and voice, and
the friendship grows once met): Mr Dixon, Auntie Edna, Granny Maggie, Mrs Birkett, Shepherd Jack. Each keeps to the
villager's file: Dixon's few words and plain prices, Edna's radio and her muddled news, Maggie's kettle and Poppy and
Tibbs, Mrs Birkett's "love" and her walkers, Jack's Herdwicks. The others stand in by their name (Ryan,
Darren) or a title (Councillor, Nurse, PC, Mr), with `voice` for their sprite's gender and `who` as their role in the pair;
no stand-in's last name is a cast id (Cast.find). No Kotlin change was needed.

**What people say here.** The characters speak as grown-ups in Cumbria do: Edna's "We was up half the night", Dixon's and
Jack's "summat", "aye", "Now then", Maggie's and Mrs Birkett's "love". The learner's phrases are standard British
English. The tutor's English notes (companion/tutor/languages/en.md, "Spoken, never counted") now name the North's and
Cumbria's spoken forms (*we was*, *it were*, *I seen it*, *I'm sat here*) beside the dialect words, as the grading
table's `nonstandard` does, so a learner who says them back is never corrected for it; a learner's own mistake (*she go*,
*goed*, *if I would have*) stays one, and the dev tutor's B1 correction is that one ("after if, no would").

## How

**Offered by level:** talks-sl's `ScenarioStore.offered(child, level)` and `talkLevelOffered` (eab33206c), used as they
are: an A1 learner is offered the eight to A2, an A2 learner the B1 ones too, a B1 learner all twenty. Nothing built
again.

**Older apps.** No format change: the twelve are `lani.scenario/v0` as the eight are. The English village is served only
English talks; Teo's tablet (0.2.698) is in the Italian village and never gets them.

**The tutor's brief** is the scenario's (`scenarioBrief`: title, role, setting and goals in English, the opener, `level`,
`language: en`, `packs`); the role says how the character speaks and pushes back.

**The dev bridge** answers a B1 or B2 English talk in lines that fit any of the twelve (`ABOVE_A2.en`, which
`ROLEPLAY_LEVELS` takes for both levels: "I see. And what happened then?", "Right. And why's that, then?", "Aye, that makes
sense.", "Grand. Thanks, that's what we'll do.", a correction of *would* after *if*), picked by the brief's level; an A1
or A2 talk keeps its own.

**The supplement** (`companion/lexicon/en.extra.json`): every word of the twelve's lines, phrases and English texts a tap
meets is known (a script over the files against the supplement's forms, the packs' words and the cast's names: 102 words
were unknown, 0 now): 90 entries and 13 forms of existing ones (explaining, leading, worrying, parked, Friday's, sooner,
meals …). Where packs-en wrote the same lemma (invite, negotiate, sign …), the merge joined the two entries (the kit's
supp-merge.py) and every form of both stayed.

- A learner of English: Home's "🗣️ Talk"; the list shows the B1 talks once the learner's level is A2, all twelve at B1; a
  talk, Start.
- The tutor: `publish_scenario` as before; a talk the tutor writes is offered at any level.
- QA: `LANI_DEV_DATA=$(cd companion/bridge && bun test/fixtures/make-learner.ts <dir> --target en --base it --name Giulia
  --level B1) LANI_CULTURE=lakeland LANI_QA_SETUP_PAIR=en-it LANI_QA_LEARNER=Giulia LANI_QA_TALK=a-gate-from-the-smithy
  LANI_QA_TALK_SHOTS=<dir> companion/bin/qa-slot --steps lakeland-talk`. A B1 or B2 talk takes the dev tutor's lines above
  A2 (VILLAGE_TALKS' `levels`, as talks-it's); at A1 a B1 talk isn't offered and the step is skipped.
