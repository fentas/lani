# Built: the Carinthian village's third wave, part b (kaernten-projects-b, 2026-11-01)

Jan, 2026-11-01 ("can you make app complete - all what is missing?"), wave B, batch 1, section 5–6 of the round's brief.
Kärnten, the German village in the lower Gailtal below the Dobratsch, had a scene for every building since places-kaernten
(docs/plans/05-built/places-kaernten.md), but its thirteen village projects had none of their own, no project steps (each
was played as the practice it always was) and no Carinthian look on their arts; its wood, stream bank and pond had no scene,
so a tap there opened nothing. This branch is part b: six projects, the stream bank and the pond. Part a
(`kaernten-projects-a`, at the same time) has the maypole, the bridge, the mill, the Stockschießbahn, the wayside chapel, the
orchard, the bee meadow and the wood. The rules are companion/SCENES.md's ("Scenes of a culture pack", "Project steps",
"Culture looks", "Seasons"); its rows for the new scenes and looks are in.

Two helpers wrote the six project scenes and their steps (`kb-places-1`: the fire station, the playground, the lookout tower;
`kb-places-2`: the fountain, the church clock, the Doppelharpfe), in this worktree, each committing its own files; the spots,
the looks, the bridge's fix, the tests, the docs and the QA are this branch's own.

## The bridge: a German noun tested by a step's turn

`check-steps` (and `validateCulture`, through project-steps.ts) could never count a German noun as one of a step's tested
words: the pack's word with its article ("der Trog") split into two words, and a turn counts a word of one; and with no
German dictionary a verb counted only in its exact form. `testsWord` now leaves the article out (de, it, en, as lexicon.ts's
ARTICLES) and, for a language without a dictionary, guesses the forms by the stem and the pack's plural; Slovene counts only
the dictionary's forms, as before (commit ac44368f, with a smoke check; part a's 1e58bcda keeps the flag in check-steps'
wrapper). Part a and the Lake District's branches take the same commits.

## How to use

Nothing to switch on. A Carinthian village's bridge serves the scenes and the steps as soon as the node's checkout has them:
a finished project's landmark has its card's "🚪 Hineingehen · Go in", the stream bank and the pond theirs, and today's step
of a project is played as a short scene where it happens. The I spy clues and the looks come with the next app build. An
older app plays the scenes in the Slovene picture (no new art, slot or effect) and an older bridge serves the projects'
packs as plain ones.

To look at the pictures: `./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest --tests '*KaerntenPlacesLooksTest*'`, then
`app/build/scene-snapshots/looks/kaernten-places/`: `<art>.png` (Primorska on the left, Carinthia on the right, by day and in
the evening; the fountain's and the Doppelharpfe's every stage a row), `<art>-k2|k3-<culture>.png` closer up,
`-night-winter`, `-october-dusk`, `-december`, `-phone-rain`.

On the emulator: `LANI_CULTURE=kaernten LANI_DEV_DATA=<make-learner.ts --target de> LANI_QA_PAIR=sl-en companion/bin/qa-slot --steps kaernten-projects-b`.
