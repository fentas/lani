### For a native speaker

**Wrong choices that must be a learner's mistake, never something grown-ups say** (the rule of followups-12.md). Each
was written as one; please strike any that people do say, and it becomes a second right answer:
- **Mixed conditionals** (one half bi, the other bom or the present): *Če bi pihala burja, se bom vrnil prej*, *Z veseljem
  bom igral, če bi mi pokazali*, *Jaz bom vesel, če bi prišel gor*, *Mogoče bi verjeli, če jim bo kdo pripovedoval*,
  *Če bi letos pripravili šest vozov, bomo mirni*, *brez stroja bodo vaše roke …*; the indicative for what didn't
  happen: *Če ste zmagali / vedeli / mešali, …*, *Če si šel, bi bil …*, *Če so bili res volkovi*, *Sicer je ostal
  neznan kmet*, *Na vašem mestu ji dam malico*, *Če sem jaz Muri*.
- **Reported speech**: the tense shifted back (*da je bila cesta zaprta*, *da je bil podoben mizi*, *da je pod mostom
  živel škrat*, *da je juha pomagala*); *da* for a request (*Rekli so, da ljudje jutri ostanejo doma*, *mi je govoril, da
  ne hitim*: is *da* for a command heard in speech?); *vprašaj, da lahko dobiš*; the person (*naj jutri ostanem doma*,
  *ali lahko dobim*).
- **Clitics**: *Rad šel bi*, *Jaz bi obdržal ga*, *Jaz bi ne bil zdržal*, *bi bili si prihranili*, *Kdo si bi mislil*.
- **Feelings**: *Malo sem strah*, *Vam ne zebe v roke?* (also v-hisi's *Vam ne zebe?*), *Če ste slabo*, *Zelo me je
  lačno*, *Seveda vas je žal*.
- **Cases and forms**: *kljub gnečo*, *ob burjo*, *za Tinetom* (for whom), *pri mletja*, *pri obeh stebrah*, *z dvema
  vedri*, *dvema prijateljem*, *Dajte obe*, *Dva snopa so ostala*, *Dva snopa je ostalo celih*, *Potem manjkajo / manjka
  še dva*, *bi bili oba padli*, *tri uri*, *za tri ure* (how long), *polovico sestavek*, *več cvetje*, *Lahko jih
  poveste*, *Tebe bi kupil bonbone*, *pomagal mamo*, *Kako jih pa ločite* (twins), *sta vam vlekla za nos*.
- **Agreement and participles**: *semena razdeljeni*, *semena razdelila*, *moka zmleto*, *moka zmlela*, *ko bo požet /
  požel* (žito), *kurjenje … je prepovedana*, *posekana hrast*, *hiša razpokan*, *Pojoča?* (the gerund),
  *poklical* (the empress), *Treba sem biti miren*.
- **Relatives and pronouns**: *v ki*, *v katerem* (shoes), *tisto, ki*, *kar jih je všeč*, *jajca, ki ga imam*, *ki ga
  pozna pot*, *ki so reševalci pripeljali*, *čigava delo*, *Nekdo ne bo zaspal*, *Nihče bo zaspal*, *se lahko izgubi
  nihče*, *če bi jim nihče pripovedoval*.
- **Clauses**: *zato je veliko dela*, *zato je burja podrla drevo*, *čeprav čebele potrebujejo hrano*, *čeprav je v jamah
  vreme enako*, *ker je včasih nevarno*, *da je vino še boljše*, *ne da se je premaknil*, *dokler se vrneš*, *Kljub temu
  ni bil kapitanov, je lep*, *Četudi …, zato je lep*.
- **Aspect, comparison, words**: *Ves dan bom pomogel*, *Ves dan sem naredil*, *ga je bila voda že odnašala*, *kje si jo
  končno iskal*, *bolj dober*, *bolj prej*, *Vprašajte* for *vprašajva*, *ne puščaj* on a sign.
- **Idioms with a word swapped**: *račun brez kuharja*, *držim palce* (German's thumbs: is it heard in Primorska?),
  *iz miši slona*, *psa v žaklju*, *kot po medu*, *popiti, kar si skuhal*, *umazano posodo*.
- **From the other scenes** (the helpers' lists): *Vam ne zebe?*, *da vam še danes jezi*, *Zdi se me*; *Najprej vas bi*,
  *če ženin bi*, *ki jih sem*, *kdaj je jo*, *zakaj zamudili ste*, *ki ga sem*, *Potem je treba zamenjati ga*, *Ko ste
  prišli, seno je bilo že zgnilo?*; *pastirje je treba pomagati*; *Marsikdo bi bili ostali*; *nikoli pozabi*; *Francu*
  for *Francetu* (is *Francu* heard, from *Franc*?); *kravom*; *Naj vam ne skrbi*; *Je povedal, kdaj bi prišel?*; *zelo

  *mlinarjom*, *dolžinov*.

**Where** each variant goes is the guide's: in the scene's `variants`, its id `<happening>-b1-<topic>` or
`-b2-<topic>`, first in its happening's `dialogs` (B2, then B1, then what it had; the happening's `dialog` stays the one
Jan heard before, last). Nothing that was there changed: no line, no variant, no word, so no voice clip goes.

**How long, what it does.** B1: 5–7 lines, two turns, a line up to about 140 characters; it leaves the here and now
(why, what someone said, what would happen if, a plan, the learner's view with a reason, a small problem). B2: 6–8
lines, two or three turns; it weighs and concedes, regrets and speculates about the past, shifts register, uses an
idiom; at least one turn of each B2 dialog tests understanding, its why in meaning words and no page. The person speaks
first and last, and answers every turn.

**Pages.** A B1 dialog's wrong choices name pages of A1–B1 only, a B2 dialog's A1–B2 (the model's
IntroductionContentTest asks a fresh B1 learner for every rule of the dialogs they meet). Every levelled dialog names at
least one page; the one-word wrong choices' English whys use the words the app's guess knows (`Forms.rule`: "Ker is the
cause", "In reported speech", "the relative pronoun", "doubles the no"), so a scene served without its pages still has
them guessed (FormsTest: 864 of 949 form choices guessed, 91.0 %; of the levelled ones 163 of 175, the rest wrong choices of meaning or an idiom).

**Never what people say** as a wrong choice: no spoken form (a for ali, bojo, the short infinitive, polvikanje *ste
bila*, the spoken hour), no clitic-first sentence, no *kateri* for *ki* in the nominative, no partitive *kislega vina*
(found and taken out in v-kleti), no *tri vrata* (stevniki: heard, the standard is *troje vrat*).

**The learner** is a gender pair wherever a form agrees with them (`{m:…|f:…}`, in Italian too), a turn's choices never
read the same for a man and a woman, and a wrong choice that differs in the paired word has its own pair. The learner
says vi to Micka, Ančka, Anton, France, Janez, Jože, Mojca, Tone and Vida, ti to Luka, Marko and the children; a vi to a
woman takes the plural l-form.

**The cast** as the scenes have them, their facts kept (VILLAGERS.md and each villager's `story`: Janez the old
forester and his late Ana, Vida's inn in Trnovo, Ančka's Radio Koper, France's mill project and Sivko, Luka's Bela who
keeps getting lost). The village is founded in play: a scene opened from a project or a building (the bridge, the
toplar, the lookout tower, the watchtower) dates nothing of its own before it was built; the past is where the person
lived before (the Trnovo plateau, the valley, Gorica, Trst). A variant that names another villager of the scene waits,
as every variant does, until they are in the village and met.

**No new cues**: no `sky` (ScenesContentTest lists every dialog that keeps a sky), no `fx`, no `act`; no "glej" or
"look" without a cue (SceneCoherenceTest).
