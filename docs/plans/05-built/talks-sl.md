Built 2026-11-03 on branch `talks-sl` (followups-16.md, section 1, Slovene). The free talks ("🗣️ Pogovor · Talk":
role-plays where the tutor plays someone in a real situation) of Jan's village were six, all A1. Jan aims at B2. Now
there are six more at B1 and six at B2, for a grown-up, set in Primorska, with every text Jan reads in English, German

or B2 one. Everything written here is machine-written and needs a native check (the list comes first).

**What a B1 or B2 talk does that an A1 one doesn't.** A1: one need, the present, short answers ("Boli me grlo"). B1:
something happened (the past, with the learner's own gender: "{m:Kupil|f:Kupila} sem jih pred dvema tednoma"), a
problem to explain and a wish to argue for (the conditional: "Raje bi {m:imel|f:imela} nov par", "Bi lahko {m:prišel|f:prišla}
v petek?"), advice ("Na tvojem mestu bi …", "Lahko bi …", "Zakaj ne bi …?"), the character pushing back once. B2: an
argument with its reasons and concessions ("Razumem, da …, a …"), a sequence told precisely (when, where, who had right
of way), hypotheses ("Kaj pa, če bi …?"), a formal register kept (the mayor, the interviewer, the claims adviser), a
negotiation that moves in steps and ends in a summary. Every character says vi, but the neighbour and Marko (ti), as in
the village.

## How

**Offered by level** (commit eab33206c, language-neutral: the other `talks-<lang>` branches can take it as it is):
`ScenarioStore.offered(child, level)` drops a curated talk more than one level above the learner's
(`talkLevelOffered`): an A1 learner is offered A1 and A2, an A2 learner up to B1, a B1 learner up to B2, a level the
bridge doesn't know counts as A1. One above, not none: a talk is chosen from a list, not met on the way, and the tutor
plays it at the learner's level ("answer in simple Slovene at Jan's level"), so it is a stretch the learner takes on
(i+1), and every talk an A1 learner has today stays (they are all A1 or A2). The tutor's own talks are offered at any
level, as their modules are. `GET /scenarios`, `GET /scenarios/:id` (404 above) and a scene's "keep talking"
(`talksOffered`) pass `learnerLevel`. The filter is the bridge's, so an app of any version gets it at once.

**Older apps.** No format change: the twelve are `lani.scenario/v0` as the German ones are (title, goals and who per
language, texts per base, `audience`, `packs`, `place`, `speaker`), served as strings in the pair. Teo's tablet (0.2.698)
never gets them (an Italian village is served only Italian talks), and a child is never offered a grown-up's.

**The tutor's brief** is the scenario's as before (`scenarioBrief`: title, role, setting and goals in English, the
opener, `level`, `language: sl`, `packs`): the role says how the character speaks and what they push back on, the level
how they speak.

**The dev bridge** answers a B1 or B2 talk in lines that fit any of the twelve (`ROLEPLAY_LEVELS.sl`, one set for B1 and B2: "Razumem. Kaj pa
potem?", "Zanimivo. Zakaj pa?", "Prav, to se mi zdi smiselno.", "Dobro, hvala. Tako bomo naredili.", a correction with
the conditional), picked by the brief's level; an A1 talk keeps its soup.
