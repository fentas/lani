## The numbers

"Words learned" counts the vocabulary cards a learner has right (GAME.md, "Words learned per age"); a pack word whose
spelling the learner already has is one card (packs.ts `learnedWords`). So what counts is the **distinct** words of the
curated packs a German learner gets: `companion/packs/de` and `cultures/kaernten/packs`, compared as packs.ts's
`normWord` (lower case, no punctuation, the article kept; a word marked homonym once per meaning).

## How packs reach a learner (checked)

With packs-en's fixes, a German learner at A1 gets the A1 packs first, then A2, then B1, nearest level first; the packs
two levels or more above wait under "For later"; an older app (`?packs=1`) at A1 gets the A1 and A2 packs only. The gap
the first writer reported (a B1 pack with one word the learner had elsewhere counting as started and being suggested
before the A1 packs) is closed by 9e07e082: above the learner's level a pack is started only by a word learned in it
(`own`). The smoke's packs checks and the Android PackSessionTest cover it.
