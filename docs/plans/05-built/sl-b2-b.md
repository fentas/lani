# Built: B2 of the Slovene school, units 7–12 (sl-b2-b)

*Built 2026-11-01, branch `sl-b2-b`. Jan: "can you make app complete - all what is missing?" The outline
(companion/course/sl/course.json) had B2 as 18 planned units; three branches write them side by side (`sl-b2-a` units
1–6, this one 7–12, `sl-b2-c` 13–18). The format and how a unit is written: companion/COURSE.md (its new section "B2,
units 7–12"). Everything here is machine-written (Claude) in Slovene, English, German and Italian, settled against
fran.si and Sloleks where a form was in doubt, and waits for a native speaker's check (below).*

## What

Six units of B2, each a guided intro of five steps (Mojca's lines with the Slovene in bold for her voice, two examples
a step, the table growing, a quick question each), three worksheets and a test, set in the village's adult life
(Granny Micka's kitchen, a Saturday in the village, the miller's letter, the evening before Marko's wedding, Nejc's
errand, Mojca's school). They assume A1–B1 and B2's units before them in the outline.

- **pomanjsevalnice**: feminine -ica (and k, g, c → č, ž, č: ročica, knjižica, ovčica), -ka (žlička, lučka), masculine
  -ek, -ček, -iček (nožek, stolček, kozarček, konjiček), neuter -ce, -ece, -esce (okence, mestece, drevesce) and sonček
  that turns masculine; the cases by the little word's own ending, the e of -ek dropping (na stolčku, Janezka); what
  a little word means: small, a little of (košček, kapljica), dear (mamica, kuža), a word of its own (žlička, konjiček,
  zvonček, lisička, mlinček), skodelica simply a cup, and *Ste za kavico?*
- **izpeljanke**: who does it (-ar, -ač, -ec, -telj: the villagers' own titles, mlinar, kovač, lovec, učitelj,
  pripovedovalec), a woman (-ka, -ica; pek has only the old pekovka), where (-arna, -nica, -lnica, -išče), a thing that
  does it (-lnik, -lka), what it is like (-ost, -ota, -ina), of it (-ski, -ni, -en, -it) and who lives there (-čan); one
  root, a family: peči → pek, pekarna, pecivo, pekovski.
- **predpone**: pisati's prefixes and their meanings (prepisati, podpisati, opisati, vpisati, zapisati), direction
  (pri-, od-, pre-, raz- on nesti and peljati), iti under a prefix (pridem, odšel, ušel, obšel, našel), the new
  imperfective for what goes on or recurs (prepisovati, podpisovati, prinašati, raznašati, prihajati, odhajati), and
  po-, na- … se, za- (posedeti, najesti se, zaplesati).
- **brezosebno**: treba je + the infinitive, its tenses and its no (treba je bilo, treba bo, ni treba), mogoče,
  potrebno, dovoljeno, prepovedano, škoda je, the impersonal se (Kako se pride do mlina?, se je plesalo, govori se, ne
  da se, ne da se mi), the weather and the day (grmi, dani se, se je zdanilo); the past always -lo.
- **predlogi-dva-sklona**: the case by the meaning: kam/kje (pod stol, pod stolom; pod mizo twice), po + accusative to
  fetch / + locative around and after, ob + locative by and at / + accusative against and losing (krava je ob mleko),
  za + accusative for and how long / + instrumental behind and after, pred and med + instrumental for time, v and na +
  accusative for days and how often.
- **stevniki-napredno**: how many times (dvakrat, večkrat, prvič, zadnjič; 2-krat), what kind (enojen, dvojen,
  trojen), dvoje, troje, četvero with plural-only nouns and pairs (dvoje vrat, troje hlač, dvoje čevljev), numbers as
  nouns (the school's grades enka to petica, dvojčka, dvojica, polovica, tretjina, četrtina, pol), the numbers declined
  after a preposition (s tremi, pri štirih, s petimi) and po for each.

the twist).

## The grading B2 needs

- **A prefix one letter off another is another verb** (`grading/sl.json`, new `wrong` list, as Italian's): prinesti
  against prenesti, priti against preiti, pripisati against prepisati, napisati against zapisati, oditi against obiti
  are one letter apart, and the stem's typo rule forgave *prenesel* for *prinesel* as almost. Only whole forms of
  those roots are listed, so a slip inside the root (*prinsel*, *prepisval*) and a missing č, š, ž stay almost.
  GradingLanguagesTest checks both sides; README's grading line names it.
- **Spoken and less right forms are accepted, never wrong**: *tri vrata* (SP2001 °, the standard *troje vrat*) is an
  `almost` choice; *štirka* beside *štirica* (both SP2001) is right; a figure SP2001 allows (*3-krat*) is accepted
  beside the word; a choice's literal reading that doesn't fit (*a little horse* for konjiček in *Moj konjiček je
  ribolov*, *little bells* in *šopek zvončkov*) is almost.
- Every answer of the six units is graded right by the app's own grading and no bank's distractor is right or almost
  for any gap (CourseContentTest).

## The dictionary

The units' words the dictionary lacked (114 of 205 by its readings: the little words, the derived nouns, the prefixed
verbs, the numerals) are in the supplement (`companion/lexicon/sl.extra.json`): 104 new entries, 98 of them with their
standard forms from Sloleks 2.0 (its non-standard forms left out), the six Sloleks lists otherwise (enojen, trojen,
dvoje, troje, mestni, pol) by their lemma; each with a gloss and its meanings in German and Italian, citing its SP2001 entry
and its Sloleks headword with URLs (8 more were in the supplement already). Two glosses the dictionary got wrong for
these units are overridden, as babica's is: *potreben* (its only sense was a slang one; SP2001: necessary, in need)
and *lovka* (only "tentacle"; SP2001 has the huntress too). *lučka* (only a name) and *mlinar* (only a surname) got
their nouns; *zaleteti*, *spotakniti* and *sprehoditi* (the verbs of zaleteti se …) their verbs. The printed sheets' "Besede" now show them.
