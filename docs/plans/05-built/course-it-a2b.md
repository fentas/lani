Teo (a child of about seven, learning Italian from German in the Friuli village; a tablet) finished A1 and found A2
outlined in companion/course/it/course.json: 18 units marked `"planned": true`. Three branches write them side by side:
`course-it-a2a` units 1–6 (riflessivi … superlativi), this branch (`course-it-a2b`) units 7–12, `course-it-a2c` units
13–18. The format and how a unit is written are companion/COURSE.md ("The Italian course: A2, units 7–12" has the
summary); A1's way is docs/plans/05-built/course-it-b.md and course-it-c.md.

Each is the size of A1's units: a guided intro of five steps (Maestra Elena's lines in German first, then Slovene and
English, "du"; every Italian form in bold with its meaning in brackets after it, for her voice; two examples a step; the
table growing row by row; a quick question each, three choices and two gaps), three worksheets from easy to harder (the
forms first, then a gap text with a word bank, then a transform list), and a test at pass 80 with new sentences.

- **passato-essere**: io, tu, lui/lei with -o or -a (Lorenzo: «Sono andato …», Giulia: «Sono andata …»); noi, voi, loro

  coming and staying, with the German "bin, ist" as a guide (Tommaso è caduto); stato for essere and stare; the reflexive
  verbs in the past (mi sono lavato, ci siamo divertiti). The gap text: Giulia's Saturday at the market in Cividale
  (the ponte del Diavolo, the gubana). The test: Davide's sheep on the hill.
- **participi-irregolari**: families that sound alike, -tto (scritto, rotto), -so (messo, chiuso, perso, acceso),
  -sto and -erto (chiesto, risposto, aperto), -nto (vinto, spento) and bevuto, venuto; with essere agreeing (è venuta,
  sono rimaste, è scesa). The gap text: Lorenzo's sixth birthday (Grandpa Franco's candles, Moro at the party). The
  test: Nonno Bepi's story under the linden.
- **imperfetto**: C'era una volta, ero … erano, c'era and c'erano; -avo, -evo, -ivo; facevo, dicevo, bevevo and the

  streams. The test: young Nonno Bepi's house by the river. It asks for the forms only; the choice of the two pasts is
  the next unit's.
- **imperfetto-passato**: the picture and what happens in it (Dormivo quando è arrivato Moro), mentre, the words that
  decide (sempre, ogni giorno, di solito, da piccolo against un giorno, all'improvviso, poi; ieri only says when: Ieri
  pioveva), a story's picture first, a counted time (per due ore, tre volte). For the Slovene base the aspect is the hint (spal, prišel). The gap
  text: the night of the bora at Nonna Rosa's. The test: a Sunday's storm in the square.
- **futuro**: -ò … -anno with -are's a → e and the accent, -ire keeping its i, the short ones (sarò, avrò, andrò, farò;
  potrò, dovrò, vedrò; verrò, vorrò, berrò), giocherò, cercherò and mangerò, plans and promises, Zia Nives's forecast (Domani pioverà),

  harvest. The test: Zia Nives's radio and a weekend at the sea.
- **pronomi-diretti**: lo and la before the verb, the Italian gender counting (la palla → la, "der Ball" in German); li
  and le; mi, ti, ci, vi (Ti aspetto, without German's "auf"); non lo so, voglio vederlo or lo voglio vedere; l' before
  avere and the participle agreeing (l'ho presa, li ho mangiati, le ho viste). The gap text: Lorenzo looking for his
  things before the trip. The test: Nonno Bepi looking for his glasses (they're on his nose).

## The grading A2 needs

`grading/it.json` and the app's `Grading`/`Spelling` (data-driven, a table per language):

- **A participle that doesn't agree is wrong** (Giulia è andato, siamo arrivate, l'ho visto for l'ho vista, li ho preso):
  it was already, by the ending rule; `GradingLanguagesTest` pins it.
- **lo or la not elided before avere is almost only where the participle agrees**: the loose rule wrote any lo or la
  before a vowel or h as l', so "lo ho vista" for "l'ho vista" (the pronoun's gender against its participle) passed as
  an apostrophe slip. Now "la ho vista" and "lo ho visto" are almost (apostrophe), "lo ho vista" and "la ho visto" wrong;
  già, mai, sempre … between are skipped; the article as before (la acqua, lo hotel).
- **A known mistake is never a typo**: a new optional key of the table, `wrong`, lists words that are grammar mistakes one
  letter off the right word, regular expressions matched whole against a word as `fold` writes it. Italian's: the regular
  stem where a form has its own (anderò, averò, vederò, poterò, doverò, saperò for andrò …; ferò, sterò, derò for farò,
  starò, darò; mangierò, comincierò for mangerò, comincerò; the conditional's alike, anderei, for course-it-a2c's vorrei;
  venito, bevito, morito, piacito for venuto, bevuto, morto, piaciuto). Before, one letter off, they passed as a slip in the
  stem, half an item, for the very thing futuro and participi-irregolari teach.
- **Accents and apostrophes stay almost**, as in A1 (andro for andrò, e andata for è andata). A slip in another word's
  stem stays forgiven (giocerò for giocherò, bandira for bandiera); no real word is in the list (venite matches the
  participle's pattern and is right where it is asked).
- Documented where grading is: the README's two passages, COURSE.md's Italian row, module-spec.md's matching rules, the
  fix skill (the tutor's guide topic "fix", regenerated with `tutor-export`), and the notes of `it.json` and `sl.json`.

## Writing rules these units follow

- **Only what comes before around a gap**: A1 and A2's units 1–6 (reflexive verbs in passato-essere, the modals in
  pronomi-diretti's voglio vederlo). passato-essere's gaps take only regular participles and A1's stato and venuto
  (restare, not rimanere: rimasto is the next unit's); imperfetto asks for its forms only.
- **Whose sentence it is**: a gap of the first person says who speaks (Giulia: «Ieri sono ___ al mare.», "Mi chiamo
  Giulia."), so the -o or -a is never the child's own and never a gender trap.
- **No sentence where both pasts would do**: each of imperfetto-passato's has the word that decides (or a state: Giulia
  era sul divano); ieri decides nothing (Ieri pioveva); its transform lists say `whole`. A quick question whose present
  would do too names its tense (Domani ___ caldo. (fare · futuro)).
- **No gap whose answer is a bare l'**: an unelided lo there couldn't be told from a slip (the gap holds only "l'"), so a
  pronoun's gap is one word and l'ho vista is typed whole in a transform line.
- **Several right answers listed**: perso and perduto; è restato and è rimasto; abbiamo bevuto and bevuto after "abbiamo
  cantato e"; la aspettiamo and l'aspettiamo; voglio vederlo and lo voglio vedere; a person in brackets left out where the passato's two words keep `drop_subject` from doing it by itself
  (Giulia è andata al mare. / È andata al mare.); "domani" before, after the subject, at the end or left out in futuro's lists.
- **Distractors that make the unit's mistake on purpose**: siamo partiti, sono entrato, abbiamo tornato; ha spegnuto, ha
  aprito, abbiamo bevito; era, cantavamo, dormivo; è arrivato (for the bora), ha cucinato, chiudeva; mangierà, veniranno,
  sarò; gli, ci. Each wrong for every gap, none almost.
- **Nothing Slovene-only**: no `lemma`/`form` (no Italian forms book), nothing turned on.

## How to use

Nothing to switch on: a Friuli village's bridge serves the course from its checkout once this is merged (GET /course,
the bridge reads the files per request), and the app's next build bundles it. The units come into Teo's school as A2
opens (the school standing and every A1 test passed), each the current one in turn; until A2's other twelve are written
the school folds them as "📝 Kmalu" and Maestra Elena says she is preparing the next lessons once the written ones are
done (COURSE.md, "What the school shows").

```
# the prints, as Teo gets them (German base)
companion/bin/school-prints --language it --level A2 --base de --png --learner Teo --gender m
# QA: a synthetic Teo, a unit of A2 end to end and its print (LANI_QA_IT_UNIT, ciao by default)
bun companion/bridge/test/fixtures/make-learner.ts <dir> --name Teo --target it --base de
LANI_DEV_DATA=<dir> LANI_ENV_FILE=/dev/null LANI_CULTURE=friuli LANI_CHILD=1 LANI_QA_SETUP_PAIR=it-de LANI_QA_LEARNER=Teo \
LANI_QA_IT_UNIT=pronomi-diretti LANI_QA_FRIULI_SHOTS=<shots> companion/bin/qa-slot --no-town --steps friuli-school-unit
```

QA's `friuli-school-unit`, as course-it-a2a extended it (`LANI_QA_IT_UNIT`), plays any Italian unit: a later level's after
"school:reveal" (A1 done, its one line), then "school:<unit>" (its level open, the units before it done), the intro, the
summary, the first worksheet, the test straight away, the unit kept done on the dev bridge, and its sheet printed through
the print dialog (fit.js in the WebView against headless Chromium, the PDF saved, A4, its pages as chosen). This branch
wrote the same before a2a's was known and dropped it for a2a's.
