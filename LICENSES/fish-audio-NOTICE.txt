What Lani does with them
------------------------

Lani's worker in companion/voice-drama3/ runs Fish Audio Drama 3 on the learner's own computer (the node) to voice
some of the villagers' clips. Neither the model weights nor the fish-speech code are part of this repository or of
the Android app; they are kept on the node.

Lani changed the Fish Audio Materials as follows. companion/voice-drama3/drama3_rocm.py changes how the upstream
fish-speech code (fishaudio/fish-speech, commit 541566932abbaebcb0a40e562a1f846738383f38) runs, at run time, so that
it runs on AMD ROCm (gfx1201):

  - the routed FP8 experts are streamed from the weight files through a cache on the GPU;
  - upstream's Triton FP8 MoE kernel is enabled on RDNA4;
  - the unit-lower triangular solve is replaced by an exact forward substitution;
  - the token embedding stays on the CPU, and lm_head is sliced to the rows the sampler uses;
  - the codec is loaded without two masks it does not use;
  - no CUDA/HIP graphs are used.

How Lani uses the output
------------------------

The clips Drama 3 makes are never used to create, train, distill or improve another speech, audio or voice model
(Gepard or any other), as Section IV(b) of the agreement requires. The voices Lani asks it for are synthetic
characters that the model makes from a written description; none imitates a real person.
